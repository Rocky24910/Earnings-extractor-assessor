RED ROBIN GOURMET BURGERS, INC. REPORTS THIRD QUARTER OF FISCAL 2026 RESULTS
Englewood, CO – November 18, 2026 – Red Robin Gourmet Burgers, Inc. (NASDAQ: RRGB) ("Red Robin" or the "Company"), a casual dining restaurant chain serving an innovative selection of high-quality gourmet burgers in a family-friendly atmosphere, today reported financial results for the fiscal third quarter ended October 4, 2026.
Chief Executive Officer Comments
“When I became CEO in April 2025, we established the First Choice Plan to address two clear priorities: strengthen Red Robin's financial foundation and improve restaurant-level operating performance. Over the past 18 months, we have made meaningful progress on both fronts," said Dave Pace, Red Robin's President and Chief Executive Officer. "During the third quarter, we completed the key steps of our financial restructuring by refranchising restaurants, using the proceeds to significantly reduce debt, and refinancing new debt at much improved terms. These actions provide greater financial stability while retaining flexibility as we refocus on our core business."
Mr. Pace continued, "In addition to securing our capital objectives, our third quarter comp sales increase of 3.9% provides confidence that our First Choice Plan for restaurant performance is working. Our progress reflects the focused and disciplined execution of our restaurant teams and contributed to a 90 basis point expansion in restaurant level operating margins. With a stronger financial foundation and improving operating trends, we believe Red Robin is well positioned to execute on its long-term priorities."

Third Quarter 2026 Commentary
•Comparable restaurant revenue increased 3.9%. This included a 1.7% decrease in guest traffic, and a 5.6% increase in average guest check, reflecting continued improvement in comparable restaurant sales performance. The average check benefited from lapping the one year anniversary of our Big Yummm value platform that launched in July 2025.
•Restaurant level operating profit margin of 10.8%, a 90-basis-point improvement from the third quarter of 2025, primarily driven by the benefits of pricing actions, cost savings, and labor efficiencies.
•Adjusted EBITDA of $7.3 million for the third quarter of 2026, reflecting increased selling expenses associated with investments in the Company's Big Yummm value platform and First Choice marketing strategy, aimed at strengthening guest engagement and driving traffic.
Balance Sheet and Liquidity
As of October 4, 2026, the Company had outstanding borrowings of approximately $100.0 million under its new credit facility and total liquidity of approximately $29.5 million, including $23.8 million in cash and cash equivalents and $5.7 million of available borrowing capacity under its revolving credit facility, net of $9.3 million in outstanding letters of credit.
On October 2, 2026, the Company completed the refinancing of its existing credit facility with a new $115.0 million credit facility, consisting of a $90.0 million term loan and a $25.0 million revolving line of credit, maturing on October 2, 2031. The refinancing, together with the completed refranchising transactions, represents significant progress in strengthening the Company's balance sheet and enhancing its financial flexibility.
Refranchising Update
During the third quarter, the Company completed the refranchising of 108 Company-owned restaurants through three separate transactions with experienced franchisees for aggregate cash consideration of approximately $89.4 million, subject to customary adjustments. The transactions included 30 restaurants sold to Evergreen Dining LLC, 61 restaurants sold to Op Burgers, LLC, and 17 restaurants sold to Kuber Oregon, LLC and Kuber Washington, LLC.
Subsequent to quarter-end, the Company completed the refranchising of six additional restaurants to Op Burgers, generating approximately $4.8 million in additional cash proceeds, subject to customary adjustments. The remaining two restaurants are expected to close before the end of fiscal 2026, following the transfer of applicable liquor licenses.
These transactions represent a significant milestone in the execution of the Company's First Choice plan, supporting debt reduction, strengthening the balance sheet and expanding the Company's franchise-operated restaurant base.
Outlook for Fourth Quarter Fiscal 2026
The Company is updating its guidance to reflect changes to its business from the impacts of the announced refranchising transactions.
As it relates to Q4, the Company expects the following:
•Comparable Restaurant Revenue growth, excluding deferred loyalty revenue, of approximately 2.75%
•Restaurant level operating profit margin of approximately 10.0%
•Adjusted EBITDA of approximately $6.0 million
The restaurant level operating profit margin and Adjusted EBITDA include certain transition related costs associated with the refranchising transactions, including approximately $1.5 million of general liability expense related to the recently refranchised restaurants. In addition, the outlook reflects the Company's current overhead structure and does not yet include the full run-rate benefit of the cost optimization opportunities associated with the higher mix of franchised restaurants. As the transition progresses, the Company expects to address these opportunities and further improve the earnings profile of the business.
Providing income (loss) from operations and net income (loss) guidance is potentially misleading and not practical given the difficulty of projecting event-driven transactional and other non-core operating items. As such, the Company does not present a reconciliation of forecasted non-GAAP measures to the corresponding GAAP measures.

Reconciliation of Non-GAAP Results to GAAP Results
In addition to the results provided in accordance with accounting principles generally accepted in the United States of America ("GAAP") throughout this press release, the Company has provided certain non-GAAP measures, which present operating results on an adjusted basis. These are supplemental measures of performance that are not required by or presented in accordance with GAAP and include the following: (i) Restaurant level operating profit, (ii) net income (loss) before interest expense, net, income taxes, and depreciation and amortization ("EBITDA"), (iii) adjusted EBITDA, (iv) adjusted EBITDA margin, and (v) adjusted net income (loss) and adjusted net income (loss) per share - diluted.
We believe that our use of non-GAAP financial measures permits investors to assess the operating performance of our business relative to our performance based on GAAP results and relative to other companies within the restaurant industry by isolating the effects of certain items that may vary from period to period without correlation to core operating performance or that vary widely among similar companies. Management believes this supplemental information will assist with comparisons of past and future financial results against the present financial results presented herein.
Restaurant Level Operating Profit
The Company believes restaurant level operating profit is an important measure for management and investors because it is widely regarded in the restaurant industry as a useful metric by which to evaluate restaurant level operating efficiency and performance. The Company defines restaurant level operating profit to be income from operations less franchise revenue, franchise rental revenue and other revenue, plus other (gains) charges, net, selling, general and administrative, depreciation and amortization, and refranchise restaurant lease expense. The measure includes restaurant level occupancy costs that include fixed rents, percentage rents, common area maintenance charges, real estate and personal property taxes, general liability insurance, and other property costs, but excludes depreciation and amortization expense, substantially all of which is related to restaurant level assets, because such expenses represent historical sunk costs which do not reflect current cash outlay for the restaurants. The measure also excludes costs associated with selling, general and administrative functions, as well as other (gains) charges, net because these costs are non-operating and therefore not related to the ongoing operations of its restaurants. Restaurant level operating profit is not a measurement determined in accordance with GAAP and should not be considered in isolation, or as an alternative, to income (loss) from operations as an indicator of financial performance. Restaurant level operating profit as presented may not be comparable to other similarly titled measures of other companies in the Company's industry.

EBITDA and Adjusted EBITDA
We define EBITDA as net income (loss) before interest expense, net, income taxes, and depreciation and amortization. Adjusted EBITDA is EBITDA, further adjusted to exclude the impact of non-operating items including changes in estimates, asset impairments, litigation contingencies, gains (losses) on debt extinguishment, restaurant and office closure costs, gains (losses) on restaurant sales, gains (losses) on refranchise transactions, severance and executive transition costs, stock-based compensation expense and other non-cash or discrete items. Adjusted EBITDA margin is calculated by dividing adjusted EBITDA by total revenues. EBITDA and adjusted EBITDA are supplemental measures of our performance that we believe give the reader additional insight into the ongoing operational results of the Company.
