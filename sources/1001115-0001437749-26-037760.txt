GEOSPACE TECHNOLOGIES CORPORATION REPORTS FOURTH QUARTER
AND FISCAL YEAR 2026 RESULTS
Houston, Texas – November 19, 2026 – Geospace Technologies Corporation (NASDAQ: GEOS) (“the “Company") today reported revenue for the fourth quarter ended September 30, 2026, of $19.6 million compared to revenue of $30.7 million for the comparable year-ago period. Net loss for the fourth quarter ended September 30, 2026 was $13.7 million, or $(1.06) per basic and diluted share, compared to a net loss of $9.1 million, or $(0.71) per basic and diluted share, for the comparable year-ago period. The fourth quarter loss included non-cash charges of $3.5 million cumulative translation loss related to the Company’s Colombian operations and $2.9 million for underutilized assets related to select nodal rental tools and manufacturing equipment.
For the twelve-month period ended September 30, 2026, Geospace Technologies reported revenue of $80.7 million for its fiscal year ended September 30, 2026. This compares to revenue of $110.8 million for the comparable year-ago period. Net loss for the fiscal year ended September 30, 2026 was $44.3 million, or $(3.43) basic and per diluted share, compared to a net loss of $9.7 million, or $(0.76) per basic and diluted share, for comparable year-ago period.
Management’s Comments
Richard “Rich” Kelley, Chief Executive Officer and President of Geospace Technologies, said, “To provide an understanding of our financial performance this fiscal year, it is beneficial to consider the global economic picture. The year remained plagued by uncertainty and delays driven by ongoing changes in trade policy, a major Middle East energy disruption, continued sanctions, and shifts in borrowing costs. These significant factors negatively impacted our ability to conduct business in our current structure.
As such, the management team has taken decisive steps to realign our operations, reduce costs, and position Geospace for a return to profitability. During fiscal year 2026, we have executed two reductions in force, an early retirement program, and cutbacks in expenses. Additionally, the challenging business conditions during fiscal 2026 contributed to non-cash charges recorded for the fiscal year of approximately $6 million of cumulative foreign exchange loss and underutilized equipment.
Our Smart Water segment experienced disappointing declines driven predominantly by decreases in orders for our Hydroconn line of smart water meter connector cables. Evaluation of broader market conditions point to slower hardware shipments, uneven project deployments, and normalization following earlier demand surges. Several major metering suppliers are experiencing near-term hardware and deployment softness, while longer-term modernization demand appears more resilient. As part of our business restructuring, we concluded a decades long relationship with an external manufacturing representative firm, who exclusively marketed the Hydroconn connectors. In fiscal year 2027, we will use internal sales and marketing resources to build demand and secure specifications of our products in utility AMI-related procurements. This approach will reduce expenses associated with representative commissions and increase our control over product promotion and increase customer intimacy. While the contribution from the Aquana brand remains modest, the products continue to grow with 51% year over year revenue increases. In the coming year, we look to build upon the growth in Aquana revenue and maximize our early efforts with white label product manufacturing to support a revenue rebound in this segment.
Our Energy Solutions segment experienced the impacts of energy market volatility and project timing shifts, increased component and shipping costs, as well as access to raw materials. Increased competition and consolidation remain challenging in the marine exploration market and have led to decreased demand for our ocean-bottom nodes. The segment focused resources on execution of sizable contracts including the Petrobras Permanent Reservoir Monitoring (PRM) program, which is expected to be shipped and product-related revenue recognized by the end of fiscal year 2027. The previously cited global conditions proved harshest for our energy sales efforts, however, we have a strong backlog in this segment going into the next fiscal year. Long-term demand forecasts should drive more favorable land exploration market conditions resulting in additional Pioneer contracts.
For the Intelligent Industrial segment, the economic headwinds were less severe but still influenced revenue timing. The biggest factor impacting revenue generation was government procurement timing at state and federal levels. After a lengthy effort, our Quantum Technology Sciences subsidiary entered into a $10.8 million contract with the U.S. Naval Surface Warfare Center Crane Division, Crane, Indiana for the Company’s Seismic Acoustic Detection and Ranging Underwater System. Similar timing delays have occurred around the sale of the Heartbeat Detector®, a vehicle security screening technology used by correctional and border agencies. Lengthy procurement cycles and complex approvals have resulted in slower than expected revenue recognition with this product line, but the outlook and demand for the technology remain extremely encouraging.
Despite this incredibly challenging year, our restructuring decisions and implementation will set the company more solidly on a return to profitability in fiscal year 2027. We concluded fiscal year 2026 with a backlog exceeding $100 million from contracts across our three segments. Contributions to the backlog encompass not only the PRM program, but contracts with the U.S. Federal government and other agencies as well as contract manufacturing customers and smart water white label commitments. The strong backlog, a well targeted acquisition strategy and restructured operations prepare Geospace well for the future.”
Smart Water Segment
Revenue from the Company’s Smart Water segment totaled $3.1 million for the three months ended September 30, 2026. This compares to $8.5 million in revenue for the same period a year ago a decrease of 63%. For the fiscal year, revenue from this segment totaled $17.2 million versus $35.8 million for the same prior year period for a decrease of 52%. The decrease in revenue for the three-month and twelve-month periods is due to decreased demand for our Hydroconn universal AMI connectors.
Energy Solutions Segment
For the three-month period ended September 30, 2026, revenue from the Company’s Energy Solutions segment totaled $10.8 million for a decrease of 31% when compared to $15.7 million from the same prior year period. Revenue from the twelve-month period was $40.9 million, a decrease of 19%, when compared to revenue from the same prior year period of $50.7 million. The decrease for the three-month period and twelve-month period is due to lower utilization and sales for our marine ocean bottom node rental fleet, partially offset by revenue recognized related to performance on our PRM contract and sales of the Company’s ultralight land node known as Pioneer.
Intelligent Industrial Segment
Revenue from the Company’s Intelligent Industrial segment totaled $5.6 million for the three-month period ended September 30, 2026. This compares with $6.4 million from the equivalent year ago period, representing a decrease of 12%. Revenue for the twelve-month period ending September 30, 2026, was $22.2 million. This compares to the prior year-ago period of $24.0 million, a decrease of 7%. The decrease in revenue for the three-month period and twelve-month period was due to lower demand for our industrial sensors, and imaging products, partially offset by an increase in security and defense revenue.
Balance Sheet and Liquidity
For the twelve-month period ended September 30, 2026, the Company used $35.1 million in cash and cash equivalents from operating activities. The Company generated $9.8 million of cash from investing activities that included $12.1 million of proceeds from the sale of rental equipment, $1.4 million in proceeds from the sale of property, plant and equipment and, offset by $3.9 million for additions to property, plant and equipment. The Company generated $7.1 million of cash from financing activities of which $7.5 million was from borrowings from the Company’s Woodforest credit facility.
On October 29, 2026, the Company entered into a Revolving Loan and Security Agreement with Amerisource Funding, Inc. (the "Amerisource Agreement"). The Company had a $7.5 million outstanding loan balance under the Woodforest Agreement, which was refinanced with borrowings under the Amerisource Agreement at closing. The Amerisource Agreement is for a three-year term and provides a revolving credit facility with a maximum availability of $25 million, subject to a borrowing base. Interest shall accrue on outstanding borrowings at the prime rate plus 3.5% per annum. The Company is required to make monthly interest payments on borrowed funds. The Amerisource Agreement is secured by substantially all of the Company’s assets, except for certain excluded property, and is guaranteed by certain of the Company’s subsidiaries. As of the date of this announcement, the Company has available borrowings of approximately $13 million.

About Geospace Technologies
Geospace Technologies is a global technology and instrumentation manufacturer specializing in advanced sensing, IOT and highly ruggedized products, which serve smart water, energy exploration, industrial, government and commercial customers worldwide. The Company’s products blend engineering expertise with advanced analytic software to optimize energy exploration, enhance national and homeland security, empower water utility and property managers, and streamline electronic printing solutions. With more than four decades of excellence, the Company’s 390 employees across the world are dedicated to engineering and technical quality. Geospace is traded on the U.S. NASDAQ stock exchange under the ticker symbol GEOS. For more information, visit www.geospace.com.
