Kimco Realty® Announces Third Quarter 2026 Results

– Portfolio Occupancy Reaches All-time High –

– Raises 2026 Outlook on Growth in Net Income & FFO –

– Same Property NOI Growth Accelerates –

 

 

JERICHO, New York, October 29, 2026 - Kimco Realty® (NYSE: KIM), a real estate investment trust (“REIT”) and leading owner and operator of high-quality, open-air, grocery-anchored shopping centers and mixed-use properties in the United States, today reported results for the third quarter ended September 30, 2026. For the three months ended September 30, 2026 and 2025, Net income available to the company’s common shareholders (“Net income”) per diluted share was $0.41 and $0.19, respectively.

Third Quarter Highlights

•
Delivered 6.8% growth in Funds From Operations* ("FFO") per diluted share to $0.47.
•
Reached record portfolio occupancy of 96.6%, while small-shop occupancy matched its all-time high of 92.9%.
•
Achieved pro-rata cash rent spreads of 31.5% on comparable new leases.
•
Grew same property net operating income* ("NOI") 4.9% year-over-year.
•
Completed the sale of The Witmer, a 440-unit multifamily building at Pentagon Centre, for $211.5 million.
•
Repurchased 6.3 million shares of common stock totaling $150 million and repurchased approximately $74 million of preferred stock.

 

"Our third quarter results reflect strong execution, with record occupancy, significant pricing power on new leases and further acceleration in same property NOI growth. During the quarter, we sold a multifamily property at approximately a 5% cap rate and redeployed the capital toward the repurchase of $150 million of our common stock at an implied cap rate in the mid-7% range, while continuing to recycle lower-growth ground leases into higher-growth grocery-anchored centers," said Conor Flynn, Kimco CEO. "We still see further opportunity to drive our results higher, including $76 million of annual base rent from leases signed but yet to commence. This positive momentum allowed us to again raise our full year guidance, which would mark the third consecutive year of FFO per share growth above 5%. We remain committed to growing earnings and creating long-term shareholder value."

Financial Results

Net income for the third quarter of 2026 was $272.5 million, or $0.41 per diluted share, compared to $130.2 million, or $0.19 per diluted share, for the third quarter of 2025. The year-over-year increase was primarily driven by $122.4 million more of gains on sales of properties, net of impairments.

FFO was $316.2 million, or $0.47 per diluted share, for the third quarter of 2026, compared to $300.3 million, or $0.44 per diluted share, for the third quarter of 2025. Gains on sales of properties, net of impairments, are excluded from the company's calculation of FFO.

Third Quarter 2026 Operating Results

•
Executed 444 leases totaling 2.7 million square feet, generating blended pro-rata cash rent spreads of 12% on comparable spaces, including 31.5% on new leases, 7.3% on renewals and 7.7% on options.
•
Record 96.6% pro-rata leased occupancy reflecting increases of 20 basis points sequentially and 90 basis points year-over-year.
•
Small-shop occupancy remained at a record 92.9%, representing an increase of 40 basis points year-over-year.

Transactional Activities

•
Sold The Witmer, a 440-unit multifamily building at the company's Pentagon Centre mixed-use property in Pentagon City, Virginia, for $211.5 million. The cap rate on this transaction was approximately 5.1%, and the company's pro-rata share of the sales price was $116.3 million.
•
Completed the sale of four Costco-anchored assets comprising two entire shopping center properties and two ground lease parcels, for aggregate sales price of approximately $127 million. The sale, which was previously announced, reflects Kimco's continued focus on optimizing portfolio growth by recycling capital from assets with lower contractual rent growth into higher-yielding investments. 1031 exchange proceeds were used to fund the acquisitions of two centers:

o
Sunshine Plaza, a 241,000-square-foot Publix-anchored center, located in a first-ring suburb of Fort Lauderdale, Florida, for $56 million.
•
Sold three shopping centers and two land parcels for an aggregate sales price of $51.8 million, of which the company’s pro-rata share was $15.1 million.
•
Acquired the leasehold interest of BJ's Wholesale Club at Home Depot Plaza in North Haven, Connecticut, for $8.5 million.
•
Kimco’s Structured Investment Program received repayments of $114.6 million, inclusive of the Pompano Marketplace repayment, partially offset by $38.8 million of new capital deployed during the third quarter. Subsequently, the company made two new structured investments totaling $48.6 million. The company continues to secure rights of first offer or refusal on the underlying shopping centers.

About Kimco Realty®

Kimco Realty® (NYSE: KIM) is a real estate investment trust (REIT) and leading owner and operator of high-quality, open-air, grocery-anchored shopping centers and mixed-use properties in the United States. The company’s portfolio is strategically concentrated in the first-ring suburbs of the top major metropolitan markets, including high-barrier-to-entry coastal markets and Sun Belt cities. Its tenant mix is focused on essential, necessity-based goods and services that drive multiple shopping trips per week. Publicly traded on the NYSE since 1991 and included in the S&P 500 Index, the company has specialized in shopping center ownership, management, acquisitions, and value-enhancing redevelopment activities for more than 65 years. With a proven commitment to corporate responsibility, Kimco Realty is a recognized industry leader in this area. As of September 30, 2026, the company owned interests in 561 U.S. shopping centers and mixed-use assets comprising 99 million square feet of gross leasable space.

(1)

Same property Net Operating Income (“NOI”) is a supplemental non-GAAP financial measure of real estate companies' operating performance and should not be considered an alternative to net income in accordance with GAAP or as a measure of liquidity. Same property NOI is considered by management to be an important operating performance measure frequently used by analysts and investors because it includes only the NOI of operating properties that have been owned and stabilized for the entire current and prior year reporting periods. Same property NOI assists in eliminating disparities due to the development, redevelopment, acquisition and disposition of properties during the periods presented and thus provides a more consistent performance measure for the comparison of the Company's properties. Same property NOI is calculated using rental property revenues (excluding straight-line rent adjustments, lease termination income, net, and amortization of above/below market rents), less charges for credit losses, operating and maintenance expenses, real estate taxes, and rent expenses, plus the Company's proportionate share of same property NOI from unconsolidated real estate joint ventures, calculated on the same basis. The Company's method of calculating same property NOI, which may differ from methods used by other REITs and may not be comparable to them, discloses with and without the impact from redevelopment projects.
