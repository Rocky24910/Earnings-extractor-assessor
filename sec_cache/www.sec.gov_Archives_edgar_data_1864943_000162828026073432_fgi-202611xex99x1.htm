EXHIBIT 99.1
FGI INDUSTRIES ANNOUNCES
THIRD QUARTER 2026 RESULTS
EAST HANOVER, N.J., November 12, 2026 – FGI Industries Ltd. (Nasdaq: FGI) (“FGI” or the “Company”), a leading global supplier of kitchen and bath products, today announced results for the third quarter 2026.
THIRD QUARTER 2026 HIGHLIGHTS
(As compared to the third quarter of 2025)
▪Total revenue of $31.7 million, -11.6% y/y
▪Gross profit of $8.7 million, -8.0% y/y
▪Gross margin of 27.6%, +110 bps y/y
▪Operating loss of $0.5 million and net loss attributable to shareholders of $0.9 million
▪Adjusted operating loss of $0.5 million1
▪Adjusted net loss of $0.7 million
MANAGEMENT COMMENTARY
Dave Bruce, CEO of FGI, stated, “FGI reported total revenue of $31.7 million in the quarter, representing a year-over-year decline of 11.6%. The quarter reflected a challenging market environment, with continued softness in housing and remodeling activity, reduced customer purchasing and delayed projects weighing on demand across several product categories. Despite these headwinds, gross profit was $8.7 million, and gross margin expanded to 27.6% from 26.5% in the third quarter of 2025, reflecting product mix management and ongoing cost discipline. During the quarter, FGI secured new toilet and vanity program awards that broaden our opportunity for future sales growth. Ongoing commercial pipeline activity also provides additional opportunities to expand business. New programs had not yet ramped sufficiently to offset the decline in the period, and we remain focused on translating awarded business into shipments. Management continued to emphasize pricing and margin discipline, balancing opportunities to rebuild volume with the profitability of new and existing business. Revenue decreased 11.9% in the U.S., 9.0% in Canada and 21.8% in Europe, reflecting the broader demand environment. Sanitaryware and shower systems declined 16.6% and 22.9%, respectively, while other revenue, primarily our Covered Bridge custom kitchen cabinetry business, grew 32.8% year-over-year. Covered Bridge continued to expand its geographies and dealer count, and Isla Porter, our custom kitchen joint venture, continues to establish relationships with the premium design community with on-trend products.”
Jae Chung, Chief Financial Officer of FGI, commented, “Revenue declined 11.6% year-over-year in the third quarter, reflecting softness in our core North American sanitaryware and shower system categories. Operating expenses remained disciplined, declining on a year-to-date basis, and we continued to advance our commercial pipeline and strengthen sales reporting and account-level performance tracking. Inventory management, expense control and operational efficiency remain key priorities. FGI ended the third quarter with total available liquidity of $6.3 million. We remain focused on converting new program awards into revenue, improving sales performance in Canada, pursuing additional commercial opportunities and maintaining cost and margin discipline as we navigate a challenging market environment. We believe the best use of our capital continues to be internal investment to attract new customers, expand existing relationships, develop new products and manufacturing capabilities, and expand into new jurisdictions.”

THIRD QUARTER 2026 RESULTS
Revenue totaled $31.7 million during the third quarter of 2026, a decrease of 11.6% compared to the prior-year period.
•Sanitaryware revenue was $19.1 million during the third quarter of 2026, a decrease from $22.9 million in the prior-year period.
•Bath Furniture revenue was $3.6 million during the third quarter of 2026, a decrease from revenue of $3.7 million in the prior-year period.
•Shower Systems revenue was $4.5 million during the third quarter of 2026, a decrease from $5.9 million in the prior-year period.
•Other revenue, primarily from Kitchen Cabinets, was $4.4 million during the third quarter of 2026, an increase from $3.3 million in the prior-year period.
Gross profit was $8.7 million during the third quarter of 2026, a decrease of 8.0% compared to the prior-year period. Gross profit margin was 27.6% during the third quarter of 2026, an increase from 26.5% in the prior-year period.
Operating loss was $0.5 million during the third quarter of 2026, compared to an operating income of $0.4 million in the prior-year period. Adjusted operating loss was $0.5 million during the third quarter of 2026 compared to an adjusted operating income of $0.4 million in the prior-year period. Operating margin and adjusted operating margin were both (1.6%) during the third quarter, compared to 1.0% in the same period last year.
The Company reported GAAP net loss attributable to shareholders of $0.9 million, or $0.49 per diluted share during the third quarter of 2026, versus net loss of $1.7 million, or $0.86 per diluted share, in the same period last year. Adjusted net loss for the third quarter of 2026 was $0.7 million, or $0.36 per diluted share, versus adjusted net income of $0.2 million, or $0.13 per diluted share, for the same prior-year period.
FGI holds earnings calls only for the second and fourth quarters, but releases results of operations via press releases and SEC filings on a quarterly basis. Inquiries may continue to be submitted to investorrelations@fgi-industries.com or by phone at 973-515-7190.

ABOUT FGI INDUSTRIES
FGI Industries Ltd. (Nasdaq: FGI) is a leading global supplier of kitchen and bath products. For over 30 years, we have built an industry-wide reputation for product innovation, quality, and excellent customer service. We are currently focused on the following product categories: sanitaryware (primarily toilets, sinks, pedestals, and toilet seats), bath furniture (vanities, mirrors and cabinets), shower systems, custom kitchen cabinetry and other


accessory items. These products are sold primarily for repair and remodel activity and, to a lesser extent, new home or commercial construction. We sell our products through numerous partners, including mass retail centers, wholesale and commercial distributors, online retailers and specialty stores.
Non-GAAP Measures
In addition to the measures presented in our consolidated financial statements, we use the following non-GAAP measures to evaluate our business, measure our performance, identify trends affecting our business and assist us in making strategic decisions. Our non-GAAP measures are: Adjusted Operating Income, Adjusted Operating Margins and Adjusted Net Income. These non-GAAP financial measures are not prepared in accordance with generally accepted accounting principles in the United States (“GAAP”). They are supplemental financial measures of our performance only, and should not be considered substitutes for net income, income from operations or any other measure derived in accordance with GAAP and may not be comparable to similarly titled measures reported by other entities. We define Adjusted Operating Income as GAAP income from operations excluding the impact of certain non-recurring income and expenses, including non-recurring compensation expenses related to our initial public offering ("IPO"). We define Adjusted Net Income as GAAP income before income taxes excluding the impact of certain non-recurring income and expenses, such as non-recurring compensation expenses related to our IPO, as well as income taxes at historical average effective rate and net income attributable to non-controlling shareholders. We define Adjusted Operating Margins as Adjusted Operating Income divided by revenue.
We use these non-GAAP measures, along with GAAP measures, to evaluate our business, measure our financial performance and profitability and our ability to manage expenses, after adjusting for certain one-time expenses, identify trends affecting our business and assist us in making strategic decisions. We believe these non-GAAP measures, when reviewed in conjunction with GAAP financial measures, and not in isolation or as substitutes for analysis of our results of operations under GAAP, are useful to investors as they are widely used measures of performance and the adjustments we make to these non-GAAP measures provide investors further insight into our profitability and additional perspectives in comparing our performance over time on a consistent basis. With respect to the Company’s expectations of its future performance, the Company’s reconciliations of guidance for full year 2026 Adjusted Operating Income and 2026 Adjusted Net Income are not available, as the Company is unable to quantify certain amounts to the degree of precision that would be required in the relevant GAAP measures without unreasonable effort.
