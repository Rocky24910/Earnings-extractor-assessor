BURLINGTON, New Jersey; November 24, 2026 — Burlington Stores, Inc. (NYSE: BURL), a nationally recognized off-price retailer of high-quality, branded apparel, footwear, accessories, and merchandise for the home at everyday low prices, today announced its results for the third quarter ended October 31, 2026.

Michael O’Sullivan, CEO, stated, “Total sales increased 8% in the third quarter, while comp store sales increased 1%. We are disappointed with this result. We saw good sales momentum through late September, but then our store traffic and comp trend were significantly impacted by unseasonably warm weather in October.”

Mr. O’Sullivan continued, “Despite comp growth coming in at the low end of our guidance range, we were pleased to deliver Adjusted EPS above our guidance. We continue to make good progress on our productivity and expense management initiatives.”

 

Mr. O'Sullivan went on, "We are maintaining our Q4 comp guidance of 1% to 3%. We are excited about our assortments in key Holiday categories. We are adjusting our Q4 EPS guidance down by $0.20 to account for higher fuel surcharges and freight costs. It is worth calling out that despite these adjustments our full year EPS growth is still projected to be in the mid-teens.”

 

Mr. O’Sullivan concluded, “We recognize that short-term issues, like warm weather or higher gas prices, can lead to a disappointing comp growth in an individual quarter, but this does not affect our huge confidence in the longer-term outlook for our business. We remain very bullish about the underlying performance and the longer-term growth trends in our business.”

Fiscal 2026 Third Quarter Operating Results

•
Total sales increased 8% compared to the third quarter of Fiscal 2025 to $2,932 million, while comparable store sales increased 1% compared to the third quarter of Fiscal 2025. 

 

 

•
Gross margin rate as a percentage of net sales was 43.7% vs. 44.2% for the third quarter of Fiscal 2025, a decrease of 50 basis points. Freight expense increased 50 basis points, while merchandise margin was flat as a percentage of sales. This includes the reinvestment of tariff related refunds received during the second quarter of fiscal 2026.
•
Product sourcing costs, which are included in selling, general and administrative expenses (SG&A), were $235 million vs. $214 million in the third quarter of Fiscal 2025. Product sourcing costs include the costs of processing goods through our supply chain and buying costs.  
•
SG&A was 34.5% as a percentage of net sales vs 35.0% in the third quarter of Fiscal 2025. Adjusted SG&A, excluding $2 million and $11 million of expenses, respectively, associated with bankruptcy acquired leases, was 26.3% as a percentage of net sales vs. 26.7% in the third quarter of Fiscal 2025. 
•
The effective tax rate was 26.0% vs. 24.1% in the third quarter of Fiscal 2025. The Adjusted Effective Tax Rate was 25.6% vs. 24.1% in the third quarter of Fiscal 2025.
•
Net income was $106 million, or $1.67 per share vs. $105 million, or $1.63 per share for the third quarter of Fiscal 2025. Adjusted Net Income was $110 million, or $1.73 per share, vs. $116 million, or $1.80 per share for the third quarter of Fiscal 2025; this also excluded $2 million and $8 million, respectively, of expenses, net of tax, associated with bankruptcy acquired leases.
•
Diluted weighted average shares outstanding amounted to 63.5 million during the quarter compared with 64.1 million during the third quarter of Fiscal 2025.
•
Adjusted EBITDA was $283 million vs. $266 million in the third quarter of Fiscal 2025, which excludes $2 million and $11 million, respectively, of expenses associated with bankruptcy acquired leases, a decrease of 20 basis points as a percentage of sales. Adjusted EBIT was $163 million vs. $167 million in the third quarter of Fiscal 2025, excluding the same amounts, a decrease of 60 basis points as a percentage of sales.

 

First Nine Months of Fiscal 2026 Results

•
Total sales increased 11% compared to the first nine months of Fiscal 2025, while comparable store sales increased 3%. Net income increased 35% compared to the same period in Fiscal 2025 to $405 million, or $6.34 per share vs. $4.68 per share in the prior period. Adjusted EBIT, excluding $14 million and $28 million, respectively, of expenses associated with bankruptcy acquired leases, was $608 million vs. $481 million in the first nine months of Fiscal 2025, an increase of 80 basis points as a percentage of sales. Adjusted Net Income, excluding $10 million and $21 million, respectively, of expenses, net of tax, associated with bankruptcy acquired leases, was $436 million, or $6.84 per share, vs. $332 million, or $5.19 per share for the first nine months of Fiscal 2025.

 

Inventory

•
Merchandise inventories were $1,925 million vs. $1,658 million at the end of the third quarter of Fiscal 2025, a 16% increase, driven by our 119 net new stores and a comparable store inventory increase of 10% compared to the third quarter of Fiscal 2025. Reserve inventory was 35% of total inventory at the end of the third quarter of Fiscal 2026 compared to 35% at the end of the third quarter of Fiscal 2025. Reserve inventory is largely composed of merchandise that is purchased opportunistically and will be sent to stores in future months or next season.

The Company ended the third quarter of Fiscal 2026 with $1,338 million in liquidity, comprised of $395 million in unrestricted cash and $943 million in availability on its ABL facility.

Common Stock Repurchases

During the third quarter of Fiscal 2026, the Company repurchased 291,499 shares of its common stock under its share repurchase program for $81 million. As of the end of the third quarter of Fiscal 2026, the Company had $137 million remaining on its current share repurchase program authorization.

Reconciliation of Non-GAAP Financial Measures

(Unaudited)

(Amounts in thousands, except per share data)

The following tables calculate the Company’s Adjusted Net Income, Adjusted EPS, Adjusted EBITDA, Adjusted EBIT, Adjusted SG&A and Adjusted Effective Tax Rate, all of which are considered non-GAAP financial measures. Generally, a non-GAAP financial measure is a numerical measure of a company’s performance, financial position or cash flows that either excludes or includes amounts that are not normally excluded or included in the most directly comparable measure calculated and presented in accordance with GAAP.

Adjusted Net Income is defined as net income, exclusive of the following items, if applicable: (i) net favorable lease costs; (ii) costs related to debt amendments and inducement charges; (iii) impairment charges; (iv) amounts related to certain litigation matters; and (v) other unusual or non-recurring expenses, losses, charges or gains, all of which are tax effected to arrive at Adjusted Net Income.

Adjusted EPS is defined as Adjusted Net Income divided by the diluted weighted average shares outstanding, as defined in the table below.

Adjusted EBITDA is defined as net income, exclusive of the following items, if applicable: (i) interest expense; (ii) interest income; (iii) costs related to debt amendments and inducement charges; (iv) income tax expense; (v) depreciation and amortization; (vi) net favorable lease costs (vii) impairment charges; (viii) amounts related to certain litigation matters; and (ix) other unusual or non-recurring expenses, losses, charges or gains.

Adjusted EBIT (or Adjusted Operating Income) is defined as net income, exclusive of the following items, if applicable: (i) interest expense; (ii) interest income; (iii) costs related to debt amendments and inducement charges; (iv) income tax expense; (v) impairment charges; (vi) net favorable lease costs; (vii) amounts related to certain litigation matters; and (viii) other unusual or non-recurring expenses, losses, charges or gains.

 

Adjusted EBIT Margin (or Adjusted Operating Margin) is defined as Adjusted EBIT divided by net sales.

Adjusted SG&A is defined as SG&A less product sourcing costs, favorable lease costs and amounts related to certain litigation matters.

Adjusted Effective Tax Rate is defined as the GAAP effective tax rate less the tax effect of the reconciling items to arrive at Adjusted Net Income (footnote (f) in the table below).

The Company presents Adjusted Net Income, Adjusted EPS, Adjusted EBITDA, Adjusted EBIT (or Adjusted Operating Income), Adjusted EBIT Margin (or Adjusted Operating Margin), Adjusted SG&A and Adjusted Effective Tax Rate, because it believes they are useful supplemental measures in evaluating the performance of the Company’s business and provide greater transparency into the results of operations. In particular, the Company believes that excluding certain items that may vary substantially in frequency and magnitude from what the Company considers to be its core operating results are useful supplemental measures that assist in evaluating the Company’s ability to generate earnings and leverage sales, and to more readily compare core operating results between past and future periods.

The Company believes that these non-GAAP measures provide investors helpful information with respect to the Company’s operations and financial condition. Other companies in the retail industry may calculate these non-GAAP measures differently such that the Company’s calculation may not be directly comparable.
