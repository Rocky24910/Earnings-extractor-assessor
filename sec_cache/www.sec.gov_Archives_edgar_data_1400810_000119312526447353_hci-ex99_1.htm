HCI Group Reports Third Quarter 2026 Results

 

Third Quarter Pre-Tax Income of $116 million

Diluted EPS of $6.45

Gross Loss Ratio of 20.7%

 

Tampa, Fla. – November 4, 2026 – HCI Group, Inc. (NYSE:HCI), reported pre-tax income of $116 million and net income of $86 million in the third quarter of 2026 compared with pre-tax income of $91 million and net income of $68 million in the third quarter of 2025. Net income after noncontrolling interests in the third quarter of 2026 was $83 million compared with $66 million in the third quarter of 2025. Diluted earnings per share were $6.45 in the third quarter of 2026 compared with $4.90 diluted earnings per share in the third quarter of 2025.

Management Commentary

"Earnings accelerated this quarter as greater operating leverage combined with modest premium growth to drive more profit to the bottom line," said HCI Group Chairman and Chief Executive Officer Paresh Patel. "Our performance illustrates that internal operating decisions can be as important as external market conditions."

Third Quarter 2026 Results

Gross premiums earned in the third quarter of 2026 were $316 million compared with $301 million in the third quarter of 2025. The increase of 5% was driven by a higher volume of insurance policies in force while average premium per policy remained relatively consistent.

Premiums ceded for reinsurance in the third quarter of 2026 were $97 million compared with $106 million in the third quarter of 2025. The decrease was a result of lower costs associated with the company’s new 2026-2027 catastrophe reinsurance programs, which began on June 1, 2026.

 

Net investment income in the third quarter of 2026 was $22 million compared with $18 million in the third quarter of 2025. The increase was primarily driven by growth in invested assets.

 

Losses and loss adjustment expenses in the third quarter of 2026 were $65 million compared with $66 million in the third quarter of 2025. Loss and loss adjustment expenses in the third quarter of 2026 included a small amount of favorable prior year development.

 

Policy acquisition and other underwriting expenses were $32 million for both the third quarter of 2026 and the third quarter of 2025.

 

General and administrative personnel expenses in the third quarter of 2026 were $24 million compared with $21 million in the third quarter of 2025. The increase was driven by additional personnel, annual merit increases, and stock-based compensation.

 

Nine Months Ended September 30, 2026 Results

For the nine months ended September 30, 2026, HCI Group reported pre-tax income of $343 million and net income of $254 million compared with pre-tax income of $285 million and net income of $212 million for the nine months ended September 30, 2025. Net income after noncontrolling interests was $231 million for the nine months ended September 30, 2026 compared with $201 million for the nine months ended September 30, 2025. Diluted earnings per share were $17.48 for the nine months ended September 30, 2026 compared with $15.47 for the nine months ended September 30, 2025.

 

Gross premiums earned for the nine months ended September 30, 2026 were $963 million compared with $904 million for the nine months ended September 30, 2025. The increase of 7% was driven by a higher volume of insurance policies in force while average premium per policy remained relatively consistent.

Premiums ceded for reinsurance for the nine months ended September 30, 2026 were $303 million compared with $308 million for the nine months ended September 30, 2025. The decrease was a result of lower costs associated with the company’s new 2026-2027 catastrophe reinsurance programs, which began on June 1, 2026.

 

Net investment income for the nine months ended September 30, 2026 was $58 million compared with $48 million for the nine months ended September 30, 2025. The increase was primarily driven by growth in invested assets.

 

Losses and loss adjustment expenses for the nine months ended September 30, 2026 were $202 million compared with $190 million for the nine months ended September 30, 2025. The gross loss ratio was 21.0% for both the nine months ended September 30, 2026 and 2025.

 

Policy acquisition and other underwriting expenses for the nine months ended September 30, 2026 were $96 million compared with $89 million for the nine months ended September 30, 2025. The increase was driven by a higher volume of premiums in force.

 

General and administrative personnel expenses for the nine months ended September 30, 2026 were $70 million compared with $61 million for the nine months ended September 30, 2025. The increase was driven by additional personnel, annual merit increases, and stock-based compensation.


Share Repurchase

On March 3, 2026, HCI Group announced a share repurchase program to repurchase up to $80 million of shares of HCI common stock through February 27, 2027. In the third quarter of 2026, HCI Group repurchased 30,721 shares for $6 million. The share repurchase program was completed on July 17, 2026, with a total of 504,330 shares repurchased for $80 million.

About HCI Group, Inc.

HCI Group, Inc. is a diversified holding company founded in 2006 which, through its subsidiaries, is engaged in homeowners insurance, reinsurance, real estate, insurance claims services, and insurance technology. The HCI Group portfolio of companies includes multiple property and casualty underwriters, exchanges, and captive reinsurers as well as a claims management business, a commercial real estate investment company, and a leading insurance technology company, Exzeo Group.

HCI Group's common shares trade on the New York Stock Exchange under the ticker symbol "HCI" and are included in the Russell 2000 and S&P SmallCap 600 Index. HCI Group, Inc. regularly publishes financial and other information in the Investor Information section of the company’s website. For more information about HCI Group and its subsidiaries, visit www.hcigroup.com. Exzeo’s common shares trade on the New York Stock Exchange under the ticker symbol “XZO.” For more information about Exzeo, visit www.exzeo.com.
