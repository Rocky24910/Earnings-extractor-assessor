Nuveen Churchill Direct Lending Corp. Announces
Third Quarter 2026 Results

Reports Third Quarter Net Investment Income of $0.38 per Share

Declares Fourth Quarter Distribution of $0.38 per Share, Consisting of a Regular Distribution of $0.36 per Share and a Supplemental Distribution of $0.02 per Share


NEW YORK, November 3, 2026 - Nuveen Churchill Direct Lending Corp. (NYSE: NCDL) (“NCDL” or the “Company”), a business development company externally managed by its investment adviser, Churchill DLC Advisor LLC (the “Adviser”), and by its sub-adviser, Churchill Asset Management LLC (“Churchill”), today reported financial results for the third quarter ended September 30, 2026.
Financial Highlights and Recent Developments for the Quarter Ended September 30, 2026
•Net investment income of $0.38 per share
•Net realized and unrealized loss on investments of $(0.06) per share
•Net increase in net assets resulting from operations of $0.32 per share
•Net asset value ("NAV") per share of $17.13, compared to $17.19 per share as of June 30, 2026
•Paid third quarter distribution of $0.38 per share on October 27, 2026
•NCDL Senior Loan Fund I LLC (the "Joint Venture") was formed on July 7, 2026. As of September 30, 2026, approximately 43.9% of the total capital commitments of the Joint Venture were called
•Declared fourth quarter distribution of $0.38 per share, consisting of a regular distribution of $0.36 per share and a supplemental distribution of $0.02 per share
•Subsequent to quarter end, repurchased 278,294 shares pursuant to the Company 10b5-1 Plan for approximately $3.0 million for the period from October 1, 2026 through October 30, 2026.
“NCDL reported solid third quarter financial results, highlighting the overall strength and stability of our investment portfolio,” said Ken Kencel, President and Chief Executive Officer of NCDL. “Our credit metrics and fundamentals remain strong, reflecting our conservative underwriting approach, as well as our well-diversified portfolio across position sizing and industry concentrations. As we look ahead, we remain confident in our ability to reward shareholders with an attractive distribution yield and total return profile.”
“We took meaningful actions to optimize and strengthen our balance sheet and capital structure in the third quarter, including a refinancing of one of our CLOs and the addition of $100 million of unsecured debt,” said Shai Vichness, Chief Financial Officer and Treasurer of NCDL. “During the quarter, we commenced operations of our joint venture, and we expect to continue to ramp the JV, which we believe will be accretive to NCDL's earnings profile and provide enhanced operating flexibility.”
Distribution Declaration and Recent Developments
The Company’s Board of Directors (the “Board”) has declared a regular distribution of $0.36 per share and a supplemental distribution of $0.02 per share, payable on or around January 28, 2027 to shareholders of record as of December 31, 2026.
For the period from October 1, 2026 through October 30, 2026, BofA Securities, Inc., as agent, repurchased 278,294 shares of our common stock pursuant to the Company 10b5-1 Plan for approximately $3.0 million.


PORTFOLIO COMPOSITION
As of September 30, 2026, the fair value of the Company's portfolio investments was $1.9 billion across 258 portfolio companies compared to $1.9 billion as of June 30, 2026 across 244 portfolio companies, well diversified across 25+ industries in each period.
As of September 30, 2026, the Company’s portfolio based on fair value consisted of approximately 87.1% first-lien debt investments, 7.6% subordinated debt investments, 3.1% equity investments, and 2.2% investments in Joint Venture. As of June 30, 2026, the Company’s portfolio based on fair value consisted of 89.6% first-lien debt investments, 7.3% subordinated debt investments, and 3.1% equity investments.
As of September 30, 2026 and June 30, 2026, the weighted average Internal Risk Rating of the portfolio at fair value was 4.4 and 4.3 (4.0 being the initial rating assigned at origination), respectively. As of September 30, 2026, there were investments in nine portfolio companies on non-accrual status representing 1.5% of total investments at fair value (or 2.6% of total investments at cost). As of June 30, 2026, there were investments in nine portfolio companies on non-accrual status representing 1.5% of total investments at fair value (or 2.7% of total investments at cost).
As of September 30, 2026, our Joint Venture's investment portfolio had an aggregate fair value of $147.5 million, across 95 portfolio companies in 22 industries, consisting of 100% first-lien debt investments, with a weighted average yield of 9.2% at cost.
PORTFOLIO AND INVESTMENT ACTIVITY
For the three months ended September 30, 2026, the Company funded $134.8 million of portfolio investments, of which $40.7 million was related to fundings to the Joint Venture, and received $192.4 million of proceeds from principal repayments and sales, of which $148.9 million was related to proceeds from sales of investments to the Joint Venture, compared to $24.8 million and $67.5 million, respectively, for the three months ended June 30, 2026.
RESULTS OF OPERATIONS FOR THE THIRD QUARTER ENDED SEPTEMBER 30, 2026
Investment Income
Investment income decreased to $43.7 million for the three months ended September 30, 2026 from $51.1 million for the same period in 2025. As of September 30, 2026 and September 30, 2025, the size of the Company's portfolio was $1.9 billion and $2.0 billion, at cost, respectively. As of September 30, 2026, the weighted average yield of the Company's debt and income producing investments decreased to 9.7% from 9.9% as of September 30, 2025, at cost. This decrease was primarily due to the net decline in base interest rates over the period, as spreads on newly originated investments have remained relatively stable.
Net Expenses
Net expenses decreased to $24.9 million for the three months ended September 30, 2026 from $29.7 million for the same period in 2025, primarily due to a decrease in interest and debt financing expenses, driven by a lower average interest rate and lower average daily borrowings.


Net Realized Gain (Loss) and Net Change in Unrealized Gain (Loss) on Investments
For the three months ended September 30, 2026, the Company recorded a net realized loss on investments of $(3.4) million, driven primarily by the amendment and restructuring of two underperforming debt positions, partially offset by a gain from the realization of one equity position, compared to a net realized gain of $1.5 million for the same period in 2025. The Company also recorded a net change in unrealized gain of $0.4 million, reflecting the reversal of unrealized losses on certain debt positions amended and restructured, partially offset by fair value declines in certain underperforming portfolio companies, compared to a net change in unrealized loss of $(4.2) million for the same period in 2025.
Financial Condition, Liquidity and Capital Resources
As of September 30, 2026, the Company had $39.1 million in cash and cash equivalents and $1.0 billion in aggregate principal amount of debt outstanding. Subject to borrowing base and other conditions, the Company had approximately $216.0 million available for additional borrowings under its revolving credit facility. As of September 30, 2026, the Company's debt to equity ratio was 1.23x (1.17x net of cash) compared to 1.29x (1.23x net of cash) as of June 30, 2026.

About Nuveen Churchill Direct Lending Corp.
Nuveen Churchill Direct Lending Corp. (“NCDL”) is a specialty finance company focused primarily on investing in senior secured loans to private equity-owned U.S. middle market companies. NCDL has elected to be regulated as a business development company under the Investment Company Act of 1940, as amended. NCDL is externally managed by its investment adviser, Churchill DLC Advisor LLC, and by its sub-adviser, Churchill Asset Management LLC (“Churchill”). Both the investment adviser and sub-adviser are affiliates and subsidiaries of Nuveen, LLC (“Nuveen”), the investment management division of Teachers Insurance and Annuity Association of America (“TIAA”) and one of the largest asset managers globally. Churchill is a leading capital provider for private equity-backed middle market companies and operates as the exclusive U.S. middle market direct lending and private capital business of Nuveen and TIAA. Churchill is a registered investment advisor and majority-owned, indirect subsidiary of TIAA.
