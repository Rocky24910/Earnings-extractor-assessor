Stran & Company Reports 32% Increase in Third Quarter 2026 Gross Profit as Gross Margin Expands to 33.3%

 

Nine-Month Net Income of $1.1 Million, an Improvement of $2.1 Million Over the Prior-Year Period

 

Conference Call to be Held Wednesday, November 11, 2026 at 10:00 a.m. Eastern Time

 

Quincy, MA / November 10, 2026 / Stran & Company, Inc. (“Stran” or the “Company”) (NASDAQ: SWAG), a leading outsourced marketing solutions provider that leverages its promotional products and loyalty incentive expertise, today announced its financial results for the three and nine months ended September 30, 2026, and provided a business update. Management will host a conference call at 10:00 a.m. Eastern Time on Wednesday, November 11, 2026.

 

Third Quarter 2026 Financial Highlights

 

●Sales: $28.1 million, up 8.2% year-over-year

 

●Gross Profit: $9.4 million, up 32.4% year-over-year

 

●Gross Margin: 33.3%, compared to 27.2% in the prior-year period, an increase of approximately 610 basis points

 

●Net Income: $35,000, compared to a net loss of $1.2 million in the prior-year period

 

●EBITDA: $0.3 million, compared to $(1.1) million in the prior-year period

 

●Stran Loyalty Solutions (“SLS”) Segment: gross profit of $3.0 million and income from operations of $0.7 million, compared to gross profit of $1.6 million and a loss from operations of $0.4 million in the prior-year period

 

●Cash, Cash Equivalents and Investments: $12.6 million as of September 30, 2026

 

Nine-Month 2026 Financial Highlights

 

●Sales: $92.7 million, up 6.3% year-over-year

 

●Gross Profit: $29.0 million, up 14.2% year-over-year

 

●Gross Margin: 31.3%, compared to 29.1% in the prior-year period

 

●Net Income: $1.1 million, compared to a net loss of approximately $1.0 million in the prior-year period

 

●EBITDA: $1.8 million, compared to $(0.4) million in the prior-year period

 

●Operating Cash Flow: $1.8 million of net cash provided by operating activities, compared to $4.8 million used in the prior-year period

 

 

 

 

“Our third-quarter results reflect the progress we are making in building a stronger, more profitable business,” said Andy Shape, Chief Executive Officer of Stran. “Revenue grew 8.2% to $28.1 million, gross profit increased 32.4% to $9.4 million, and gross margin expanded by approximately 610 basis points to 33.3%. We are beginning to see the benefits of the operational improvements and strategic investments we have made across the organization, particularly within Stran Loyalty Solutions, which is becoming a more meaningful contributor to our overall profitability, with segment gross profit increasing to $3.0 million from $1.6 million a year ago. Importantly, for the first nine months of 2026, we generated net income of $1.1 million, compared to a net loss of $1.0 million in the prior-year period.”

 

“Looking ahead, our focus remains on translating revenue growth into stronger earnings and cash flow, while maintaining the financial flexibility to pursue new customer opportunities and strategic growth initiatives. We also continue to execute our share repurchase program as part of our disciplined approach to capital allocation. During the third quarter, we repurchased approximately 109,000 shares. With an improving earnings profile, positive operating cash flow and a strong balance sheet, we believe Stran is entering its next phase of growth from a stronger financial position.”

 

Financial Results for the Three Months Ended September 30, 2026

 

●Total sales increased 8.2% to $28.1 million for the three months ended September 30, 2026, compared to $26.0 million for the three months ended September 30, 2025. Sales by our Stran segment increased 4.7% to $18.5 million, compared to $17.6 million, and sales by our SLS segment increased 15.4% to $9.6 million, compared to $8.3 million.

 

●Gross profit increased 32.4% to $9.4 million, compared to $7.1 million in the prior-year period. Gross profit margin increased to 33.3%, compared to 27.2%, an improvement of approximately 610 basis points. Gross profit for the Stran segment increased 15.4% to $6.4 million, with a gross margin of 34.4%, compared to 31.2%. Gross profit for the SLS segment increased 92.4% to $3.0 million, with a gross margin of 31.2%, compared to 18.7%, reflecting improved customer mix, effective cost management and lower tariffs, and including a tariff refund of approximately $0.2 million.

 

●Total operating expenses increased 7.5% to $9.5 million, compared to $8.9 million in the prior-year period. As a percentage of sales, total operating expenses decreased to 33.9%, compared to 34.1%.

 

●Net income was approximately $35,000, compared to a net loss of $1.2 million in the prior-year period.

 

●EBITDA was $0.3 million, compared to $(1.1) million in the prior-year period, an improvement of $1.4 million.

 

Financial Results for the Nine Months Ended September 30, 2026

 

●Total sales increased 6.3% to $92.7 million for the nine months ended September 30, 2026, compared to $87.3 million for the nine months ended September 30, 2025. Sales by our Stran segment increased 8.0% to $65.2 million, compared to $60.3 million, and sales by our SLS segment increased 2.3% to $27.5 million, compared to $26.9 million.

 

●Gross profit increased 14.2% to $29.0 million, compared to $25.4 million in the prior-year period. Gross profit margin improved to 31.3%, compared to 29.1%. Gross profit for the Stran segment increased 7.2% to $21.3 million, with a gross margin of 32.7%, compared to 33.0%. Gross profit for the SLS segment increased 39.3% to $7.7 million, with a gross margin of 28.0%, compared to 20.5%.

●Net income was approximately $1.1 million, compared to a net loss of approximately $1.0 million in the prior-year period.

 

●EBITDA was $1.8 million, compared to $(0.4) million in the prior-year period, an improvement of $2.2 million.

About Stran

 

For over 30 years, Stran has grown to become a leader in the promotional products industry, specializing in complex marketing programs to help recognize the value of promotional products, branded merchandise, and loyalty incentive programs as a tool to drive awareness, build brands and impact sales. Stran is the chosen promotional programs manager of many Fortune 500 companies, across a variety of industries, to execute their promotional marketing, loyalty and incentive, sponsorship activation, recruitment, retention, and wellness campaigns. Stran provides world-class customer service and utilizes cutting-edge technology, including efficient ordering and logistics technology to provide order processing, warehousing and fulfillment functions. The Company’s mission is to develop long-term relationships with its clients, enabling them to connect with both their customers and employees in order to build lasting brand loyalty. Additional information about the Company is available at: www.stran.com.

Non-GAAP Financial Measures

 

EBITDA is a numerical measure that the Company believes helps investors to compare its operating performance to that of other companies. “EBITDA” is defined as net income (loss) excluding interest income/expense, provision (benefit) for income taxes and depreciation and amortization expense. The Company believes EBITDA is an important measure of operating performance because it allows management, investors and others to evaluate and compare the Company’s core operating results from period to period by removing (i) the impact of the Company’s capital structure (interest income/expense), (ii) tax consequences and (iii) asset base (depreciation and amortization). EBITDA is a “non-GAAP financial measure” as defined under Regulation G under the Exchange Act. EBITDA should not be considered in isolation or as an alternative to net income, cash flows from operating activities or any other measure determined in accordance with GAAP. The items excluded to calculate EBITDA are significant components in understanding and assessing the Company’s results of operations. The Company’s EBITDA may not be comparable to a similarly titled measure of another company because other entities may not calculate EBITDA in the same manner.
