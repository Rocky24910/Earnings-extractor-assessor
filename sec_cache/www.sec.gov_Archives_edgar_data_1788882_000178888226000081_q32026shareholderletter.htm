Key quarterly metrics
Dear Root Shareholders
In the third quarter, Root delivered another quarter of strong profitability while continuing to advance the products, technology, and distribution capabilities designed to drive our next phase of growth. We generated $381 million of revenue, $14 million of net income, and $34 million of adjusted EBITDA, producing approximately 18% annualized return on equity. Since our last shareholder letter, we renewed our relationship with Carvana. We also launched the Hyundai Secure+ Powered by Root pilot, expanded into Wyoming, and completed development of our latest proprietary pricing model, which we expect will deliver our largest improvement in predictive power to date.
Personal auto competition intensified during the quarter as large carriers increased marketing, broadened underwriting appetite, lowered average premiums, and competed more aggressively for growth. We remained disciplined in Direct and deployed capital only where expected returns met our thresholds. In a cyclical insurance market, that discipline is a prerequisite for compounding value.
During the quarter, this discipline meant intentionally trading off incremental policy growth in the Direct channel when expected returns fell below our threshold.

Discipline, however, does not mean standing still. On the contrary, that discipline provides the financial strength to continue investing in the products and capabilities that we believe will create durable long-term growth. New writings across our Partnership channel grew 19% year over year and represented 52% of total new writings, up from 44% in the prior-year period. Our embedded partnerships show how insurance can fit naturally into vehicle ownership: Carvana Insurance Built with Root demonstrates the customer value of making insurance part of the vehicle purchase, while the Hyundai Secure+ Powered by Root pilot brings insurance directly into vehicle affordability. Our latest proprietary pricing model strengthens the pricing engine beneath each channel, while agentic commerce and connected-vehicle experiences can create new ways for customers to discover, buy, and use insurance.
Together, these investments represent the strategic progress necessary to grow through cycles and enter our next phase of expected profitable growth. We cannot control when market conditions will shift, but we can control how well prepared Root is when they do.
Highlights for the third quarter include:
1.Generated $381 million of revenue, $14 million of net income, and $34 million of adjusted EBITDA, while producing approximately 18% annualized return on equity.
2.Delivered a 97.2% net combined ratio, improving 4.9 points year-over-year, and a 61.1% gross accident period loss ratio, within our long-term target range of 60% to 65%.
3.Ended the quarter with 484 thousand policies in force, up 4% year-over-year and broadly stable sequentially. Gross written premium and gross earned premium were $358 million and $356 million, down 8% and 5% year-over-year, respectively.

6.Launched the Hyundai Secure+ Powered by Root pilot in 14 Phoenix-area Hyundai dealerships, offering six months of Root auto insurance at no cost to customers who purchase or lease a new Hyundai through Hyundai Capital America, for eligible customers who qualify for coverage under Root's underwriting criteria.

Scalable, Profitable Growth
Root’s growth strategy is built on five reinforcing levers: more sophisticated pricing; broader Direct reach through state expansion; scaled embedded partnerships; growing independent agent distribution within our Partnership channel; and new products and shopping experiences. Each lever runs on the same pricing, underwriting, claims, and policy infrastructure, allowing us to expand reach without recreating the operating system beneath it.
Embedded insurance at scale
Our partnerships show how insurance can fit naturally into vehicle ownership: Carvana Insurance Built with Root demonstrates the customer value of making insurance part of the vehicle purchase, while the Hyundai Secure+ Powered by Root pilot directly addresses the challenge of vehicle affordability.
In August, we renewed and extended our commercial relationship with Carvana through August 2028.
The renewal demonstrates the value of embedded insurance to customers at scale. Vehicle buyers should not have to leave a high-intent purchase to complete a separate insurance process. Carvana Insurance Built with Root makes the experience more convenient by bringing real-time pricing, underwriting, and policy issuance directly into the transaction.
The relationship also demonstrates the value of Root's technology to partners. The renewed agreement reflects the value both companies see in the integrated experience and creates a foundation to continue improving the product, integration, and customer experience.

A new approach to vehicle affordability
Insurance is an important part of the total cost of vehicle ownership. In October, we partnered with Hyundai Motor America to address that customer need through the Hyundai Secure+ Powered by Root pilot program in 14 Phoenix-area Hyundai dealerships. Customers who finance or lease a new Hyundai through Hyundai Capital America can receive six months of Root auto insurance at no cost,for eligible customers who choose to enroll and qualify for coverage under Root's underwriting criteria. This program allows us to meet customers at a highly relevant moment through a seamless digital experience and automated underwriting.
Deeper OEM integrations can position Root to build more seamless insurance products across the vehicle ownership journey, from purchase through claims, supported by richer, real-time data directly from the vehicle. They can also give us a front-row view of how connected vehicles, driver assistance, and automation are changing driving risk, allowing us to adapt our pricing and products as the technology advances.
Expanding where and how customers find Root

Following our New Jersey launch in July, we expanded into Wyoming in October, bringing Root to 38 states. Each launch expands our addressable market, while a larger footprint makes Root more useful to national partners by allowing successful distribution relationships to scale across more of the country.


Buying car insurance has never been an experience people enjoy. It is time-consuming, repetitive, and often confusing. AI agents can take much of that work off the customer’s hands, making it easier to compare price and coverage and reducing friction throughout the shopping and purchase process. As shopping becomes easier, customers can shop more often, shifting advantage away from broad brand spending and bundled relationships and toward carriers that deliver the best price, coverage, and experience.

Building The End-to-End AI Insurance System
Pricing remains the bedrock of Root. Better segmentation allows us to improve expected returns and offer more competitive rates to customers whose risk is better than the market recognizes. It also expands the population we can serve profitably, making pricing one of our most important growth levers. Recent double-digit rate reductions in Florida and Ohio demonstrate this capability in practice: when loss trends improve, our models allow us to respond quickly, improve value for customers and maintain target returns.
We completed development of our latest proprietary pricing model and began deploying it in the fourth quarter. We expect it to deliver our largest improvement in predictive power to date. It is designed to improve segmentation, customer lifetime value, and rate competitiveness, helping us identify more customers for whom Root can offer a compelling price while maintaining target returns.

Root is a technology company built to automate insurance end-to-end. We own the systems that acquire customers, price and underwrite risk, issue and service policies, and support claims routing and resolution. Because we built these systems ourselves, we have deep expertise in how pricing, underwriting, and claims work together. What we learn in claims can make our pricing smarter, and what we learn in pricing can improve our underwriting. That knowledge compounds over time.

Financials
Third quarter revenue was $381 million of revenue compared to $388 million in the prior-year period. Direct Contribution
We define direct contribution, a non-GAAP financial measure, as gross profit excluding net investment income, net realized gains on investments, acquisition expenses, which include report costs and commission expenses related to our partnership channel, and fixed expenses, which include certain warrant compensation expense related to policies originating through the integrated automobile insurance solution for Carvana’s online buying platform, overhead allocated based on headcount, or Overhead, and salaries, health benefits, bonuses, employee retirement plan-related expenses and employee share-based compensation expense, or Personnel Costs, licenses, professional fees and other expenses. Further impacts related to reinsurance are excluded, these consist of ceded premiums earned, ceded loss and LAE, and net ceding commission and other. Net ceding commission and other is comprised of ceding commission received in connection with reinsurance ceded, amortization of excess ceding commission, and other impacts of reinsurance ceded which are included in other insurance expense. After these adjustments, the resulting calculation is inclusive of only those gross variable costs of revenue incurred on the successful acquisition of business. We view direct contribution as an important metric because we believe it measures profitability of our total policy portfolio prior to the impact of reinsurance.
Adjusted EBITDA
We define adjusted EBITDA, a non-GAAP financial measure, as net income (loss) excluding interest expense, income tax expense, depreciation and amortization, share-based compensation, loss on extinguishment of debt, warrant compensation expense, restructuring charges, certain legal fees and other items that do not reflect our ongoing operating performance. After these adjustments, the resulting calculation represents expenses directly attributable to our operating performance. We use adjusted EBITDA as an internal performance measure in the management of our operations because we believe it provides management and other users of our financial information useful insight into our results of operations and underlying business performance. Adjusted EBITDA should not be viewed as a substitute for net income (loss) calculated in accordance with GAAP, and other companies may define adjusted EBITDA differently.
Encumbered Capital
We define encumbered capital as cash and cash equivalents held within our regulated insurance entities.
Unencumbered Capital
We define unencumbered capital as unrestricted cash and cash equivalents held outside of our regulated insurance entities. This amount includes borrowed funds that are subject to certain minimum liquidity covenants.

Gross Accident Period Loss Ratio
Gross accident period loss ratio, expressed as a percentage, represents all losses and claims expected to arise from insured events that occurred during the applicable period regardless of when they are reported and finally settled divided by gross premiums earned for the same period. The gross accident period loss ratio is remeasured each reporting period to reflect updated estimates of ultimate losses as they develop.
Distribution Channels
•Direct: seamless experiences driven by performance marketing and organic traffic connecting consumers directly to the product.
◦Digital. Our direct digital channel is designed to drive volume by efficiently capturing high-intent customers. We accomplish this by meeting our customers within platforms they use extensively such as search engines or select marketplace platforms where consumers are actively shopping for insurance. We deploy dynamic data science models to optimize advertising, targeting, and bidding strategies across our digital platforms, aligning customer acquisition cost to expected lifetime value of the potential customer.
◦Referral. We encourage our existing customers to spread our value proposition. Our referral channel compensates existing customers who refer new customers who subsequently complete a test drive. This channel facilitates community-based growth to those who value our fair and transparent approach to insurance. This is our lowest cost acquisition channel and an important aspect of our ongoing distribution and brand strategy.
◦Channel Media. We build consideration and drive intent through household-level targeted media channels including direct mail, social media, and digital media. We conduct experimental structured tests across media channels and geographies through a disciplined test-and-learn approach to evaluate new tactics. These efforts are intended to support expansion beyond high-intent acquisition channels while maintaining focus on efficiency and long-term value creation. We utilize these media channels to drive awareness when launching in new markets and to target prospective customers in active states.
