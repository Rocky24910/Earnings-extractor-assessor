Iambic Provides Business Updates and Third Quarter 2026 Financial Results

•
Launched Enchant® v3, a 41-billion-parameter transformer model for drug discovery and development, to drive decisions across our internal and partnership activities
•
Signed AI discovery collaborations with Bayer and AbbVie; eight total partnerships signed since 2024
•
Achieved a program milestone in Takeda collaboration, triggering a $1.0 million payment; AI-driven drug discovery collaboration was announced only nine months ago in February 2026
•
Presented updated IAM1363 (HER2) data from ongoing Phase 1/2 study at ESMO 2026: 36% systemic and 41% intracranial best overall response rates in heavily pretreated patients; registrational trial planned for 2027, subject to regulatory approval
•
Submitted IND and received a Safe-to-Proceed Letter from the FDA for the IAM217 (KIF18A) Phase 1/2 trial; IND submission for IAM-C1 (CDK2/4) is on track for Q4 2026
•
Meaningful revenue generation: YTD (through Q3 2026), collaboration revenue was approximately $24 million, where billings from collaborations were approximately $100 million; for the same period, Iambic’s total operating expenses were approximately $108 million and costs related to compute were less than $10 million
•
Strong cash position: As of September 30, 2026, the cash and cash equivalents position was approximately $252 million, where the pro-forma cash and cash equivalents position was approximately $455 million and includes net proceeds of approximately $156 million from our IPO and AbbVie upfront payments received in October 2026

SAN DIEGO – November 16, 2026 – Iambic (Nasdaq: IAM), a clinical-stage life sciences and technology company developing novel medicines using its AI-driven platform, today provided business updates and financial results for the third quarter ended September 30, 2026.

“Iambic’s mission is to make better technology for better medicines, and we believe that the past several months showed that at work across every part of our business,” said Tom Miller, PhD, Co-Founder & CEO of Iambic. “At ESMO, our updated clinical data showed the intracranial activity and tolerability for IAM1363, and we are preparing to start a registrational trial in 2027, subject to regulatory approval. We submitted the IND and have received a Safe-to-Proceed Letter for a Phase 1/2 trial for IAM217, our second wholly owned and platform-discovered candidate, with an anticipated IND submission for IAM-C1, our CDK2/4 inhibitor, close behind. We launched Enchant v3, a huge advance in the core AI technology that underpins our molecular superintelligence platform, and AbbVie and Bayer each chose to bring our platform to bear on their own discovery work through new Iambic partnerships. Our IPO gives us the resources to advance our pipeline through major inflection points and to keep scaling the platform behind it.”

 

Summary of Business Highlights

Pipeline

•
IAM1363 (HER2): IAM1363 is a selective, brain-penetrant HER2 tyrosine kinase inhibitor designed on the Iambic platform and advanced to the clinic in approximately two years.
◦
European Society for Medical Oncology (ESMO) 2026 data:
◦
Anti-tumor activity. Iambic presented data from its ongoing Phase 1/2 trial, as of the August 31, 2026 data cutoff, on heavily pretreated patients with advanced/metastatic HER2-altered cancers treated at ≥960 mg once daily (QD). Best overall response by RECIST v1.1 (systemic activity) was partial response (PR) in 36% (19/53), stable disease (SD) in 38% (20/53). Best overall response by RANO-BM (intracranial activity) was PR in 41% (9/22), SD in 55% (12/22).
◦
Durability of disease control. 15 participants had remained on study for at least 6 months, with more than half of participants still on treatment dosed at the recommended go-forward dose of 960 mg QD.
◦
Generally well tolerated. Most treatment-related adverse events were low grade. In participants dosed at 960 mg QD (n=68), 5.9% experienced a grade-3 treatment-related adverse event and none experienced a grade-4 or -5 treatment-related adverse event.
◦
2027 plans. Iambic plans to initiate a registrational study in patients with HER2+ breast cancer with study initiation anticipated in 2027, subject to FDA regulatory approval.
•
IAM217 (KIF18A): IAM217 is a brain-penetrant KIF18A inhibitor and Iambic’s second wholly owned clinical candidate developed using our molecular superintelligence platform, leveraging NeuralPLexer® to provide structural insights despite the absence of a high-resolution inhibitor-bound co-crystal structure throughout the program. IAM217 is intended for development in triple-negative breast cancer, ovarian cancer and other solid tumors with chromosomal instability.
◦
IND submitted. Iambic submitted an IND on September 30, 2026 and has received a Safe-to-Proceed Letter for its planned Phase 1/2 trial.
◦
Upcoming presentation. Iambic will present preclinical data for IAM217, including approximately 90% regression in an intracranial tumor model, at the EORTC-NCI-AACR Symposium in Barcelona, November 18–20, 2026.
•
IAM-C1 (CDK2/4): IAM-C1 is a selective dual CDK2/4 inhibitor designed to avoid CDK1, CDK6, CDK7 and CDK9, in development for HR-positive/HER2-negative breast cancer, including disease that has progressed on CDK4/6 inhibitors, as well as other cancer types. IAM-C1 was engineered on our molecular superintelligence platform to achieve a precise polypharmacological profile.
◦
IND on track for Q4 2026. GLP toxicology studies are complete, and Iambic expects to submit an IND in the fourth quarter of 2026, with Phase 1/2 initiation expected in early 2027.
◦
Upcoming presentation. Iambic will present preclinical data for IAM-C1, including activity in CDK4/6 inhibitor-resistant models, at the EORTC-NCI-AACR Symposium in November.
•
Early discovery: Iambic is advancing multiple wholly owned discovery programs against hard-to-drug and novel targets in oncology, metabolic and endocrine diseases, where Enchant and NeuralPLexer are being leveraged to accelerate discovery timelines.

 

Partnerships

•
AbbVie. In September, Iambic entered a multi-year collaboration with AbbVie to discover small-molecule therapies in immunology, neuroscience and oncology using Iambic’s molecular superintelligence platform, including Enchant v3 and NeuralPLexer. Iambic received upfront payments and is eligible for milestones and tiered royalties.
•
Bayer. In June, Iambic entered a collaboration with Bayer to apply its platform, including Enchant and NeuralPLexer, to hard-to-drug targets. Iambic received an upfront payment and is eligible for milestones and royalties.
•
Takeda. Iambic achieved a program milestone in the third quarter, triggering a $1.0 million payment. Iambic’s AI-driven drug discovery collaboration with Takeda was announced only nine months ago in February 2026.
•
Additional partnerships. Iambic continues to advance its work with Lundbeck, Revolution Medicines, Jazz Pharmaceuticals, NVIDIA and Lambda.

Platform

•
Enchant v3. In September, Iambic launched Enchant v3, a 41-billion-parameter mixture-of-experts model pretrained on 4.5 trillion tokens, spanning more than 6,000 molecular properties and 16 data modalities, including assay, molecule, peptide, biomolecular structure, biomedical image, multi-omics, clinical trial and pharmacokinetics data. Enchant v3 predicts preclinical and clinical endpoints with uncertainty quantification, and its predictive power has continued to improve with scale across three model generations. Enchant v3 is deployed across Iambic’s internal programs and partnerships, including the AbbVie collaboration.
•
Headquarters: Beginning in November 2026, Iambic is moving its headquarters to an approximately 121,000-square-foot facility on an Alexandria campus in the Torrey Pines area of San Diego. Iambic intends to use the expanded laboratory space to develop the next generation of its molecular superintelligence platform.

Third Quarter 2026 Financial Results

Since 2024, Iambic has entered eight partnerships across technology, discovery and clinical development. We believe that collaborations with AbbVie, Bayer and Takeda this year reflect the growing industry demand for AI-driven drug discovery. YTD (through Q3 2026), collaboration revenue was approximately $24 million, while billings from collaborations were approximately $100 million; for the same period, Iambic’s total operating expenses were approximately $108 million and costs related to compute were less than $10 million. Although we expect research and development costs to increase as our clinical trials expand, we believe that our partnership payments highlight our operational discipline and have been an efficient way to fund the company while serving the industry with leading technologies.

•
Cash Position: Cash and cash equivalents were $252 million as of September 30, 2026, which does not include initial public offering (IPO) net proceeds of approximately $156 million and $47 million of AbbVie upfront payments, both received in October 2026. Including IPO net proceeds and the AbbVie upfront payments, pro forma cash and cash equivalents as of September 30, 2026 were approximately $455 million.

 

•
Revenue: Collaboration revenue was $11.4 million for the third quarter of 2026, compared to $4.7 million for the third quarter of 2025. The increase was primarily due to Iambic’s discovery collaborations.
•
Research and Development Expenses: Research and development expenses were $34.1 million for the third quarter of 2026, compared to $23.1 million for the third quarter of 2025. The increase was primarily due to increased clinical and manufacturing costs, continued discovery pipeline momentum both across our internal assets as well as new partnership programs, and headcount growth.
•
General and Administrative Expenses: General and administrative expenses were $7.4 million for the third quarter of 2026, compared to $4.3 million for the third quarter of 2025. The increase was primarily due to headcount growth and public company readiness.
•
Net Loss: Net loss was $38.5 million for the third quarter of 2026, compared to $21.5 million for the third quarter of 2025. Net loss for the third quarter of 2026 included a $10.4 million non-cash charge from the change in fair value of convertible notes which converted into shares of our common stock immediately prior to the closing of our IPO.

About Iambic

Iambic’s mission is to make better technology for better medicines. By utilizing molecular superintelligence – integrating proprietary AI with automated, scalable chemistry and biology experimentation – our platform is designed to address the totality of drug discovery and development. We believe the utility of our platform is demonstrated by the discovery of IAM1363, a novel drug candidate advanced to the clinic in approximately two years, along with a diverse preclinical pipeline. Our leading AI technologies include Enchant and NeuralPLexer for multimodal endpoint prediction, designed to improve the speed, precision, and success rates of drug discovery and clinical development. At Iambic, we are building an engine that is designed to power the future of medicine. For more information, visit iambic.ai.
