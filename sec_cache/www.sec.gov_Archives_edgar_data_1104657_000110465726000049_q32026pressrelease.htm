MATERION CORPORATION REPORTS
RECORD THIRD-QUARTER 2026 FINANCIAL RESULTS AND RAISES
FULL-YEAR OUTLOOK

MAYFIELD HEIGHTS, Ohio – Materion Corporation (NYSE: MTRN) today reported record third-quarter 2026 financial results and raised its full-year outlook.

Financial Summary
•Net sales were $567.3 million versus $444.8 million in the prior year period; value-added sales1 were $313.7 million versus $263.9 million in the prior year period
•Net income of $38.3 million, or $1.81 per share, diluted, versus $25.4 million, or $1.22 per share, in the prior year period; adjusted earnings of $1.93 per share versus $1.41 in the prior year period
•Operating profit of $50.4 million versus $34.9 million in the prior year period; adjusted EBITDA2 of $70.7 million, or 22.5% of value-added sales, versus $55.5 million, or 21.0%, in the prior year period
•Strong free cash flow3 generation of ~$46 million, with 120% conversion
•Raising full-year adjusted earnings per share outlook to $7.10 – $7.40, an increase of 33% year-over-year at the midpoint

Business Highlights
•Delivered:
◦Record quarterly value-added sales and earnings
◦Highest quarterly semiconductor sales in company history
◦Double-digit year-over-year sales and earnings growth in each segment
◦Fourth consecutive quarter of year-over-year adjusted EBITDA margin expansion
•Incoming orders and backlog reached all-time high, up ~25% and ~35% year-over-year respectively
•Making significant progress towards mid-term adjusted EBITDA margin target of 23%

“Our third quarter results demonstrate the power of our advanced materials portfolio and the strong momentum building across the company. The team’s commitment to delivering for our customers across diverse end markets drove double-digit growth in each business segment and resulted in another quarter of record sales and earnings,” said Jugal Vijayvargiya, President & CEO of Materion.

“As we enter the fourth quarter, the outlook is very strong. We expect a meaningful step up in sales supported by record backlog and robust demand across our end markets. Combined with continued strong execution across the business, we are well-positioned to deliver our strongest quarter yet, closing out an exciting record year for Materion."

THIRD QUARTER 2026 RESULTS
Net sales for the quarter were $567.3 million, compared to $444.8 million in the prior year period. Value-added sales were $313.7 million for the quarter, up 19% from the prior year period, driven by record quarterly semiconductor sales and significant growth across aerospace & defense and telecom & data center end markets.
Operating profit for the quarter was $50.4 million and net income was $38.3 million, or $1.81 per diluted share, compared to operating profit of $34.9 million and net income of $25.4 million, or $1.22 per share, in the prior year period.
Excluding special items4, adjusted EBITDA was a third quarter record at $70.7 million, or 22.5% of value-added sales, compared to $55.5 million or 21.0% of value-added sales in the prior year period. The strong adjusted EBITDA and margin were driven by higher volume and strong price/mix.
Adjusted net income was $40.8 million excluding acquisition amortization, or $1.93 per diluted share, compared to $1.41 per share in the prior year period.
OUTLOOK
Our strong year-to-date performance, record backlog and continued order rate momentum have further reinforced our confidence in delivering record results for 2026. We are raising our full-year adjusted earnings per share guidance to $7.10 to $7.40, representing a 33% increase over the prior year at the midpoint.

ABOUT MATERION
Materion Corporation is a global leader in advanced materials solutions for high-performance industries including semiconductor, industrial, aerospace & defense, energy and automotive. With nearly 100 years of expertise in specialty engineered alloy systems, inorganic chemicals and powders, precious and non-precious metals, beryllium and beryllium composites, and precision filters and optical coatings, Materion partners with customers to enable breakthrough solutions that move the world forward. Headquartered in Mayfield Heights, Ohio, the Company employs more than 3,000 talented people worldwide, serving customers in more than 60 countries.

The cost of gold, silver, platinum, palladium, copper, ruthenium, iridium, rhodium, rhenium, and osmium is passed through to customers and, therefore, the trends and comparisons of net sales are affected by movements in the market price of these metals. Internally, management also reviews net sales on a value-added basis. Value-added sales is a non-GAAP financial measure that deducts the value of the pass-through metals sold from net sales. Value-added sales allows management to assess the impact of differences in net sales between periods or segments and analyze the resulting margins and profitability without the distortion of the movements in pass-through market metal prices. The dollar amount of gross margin and operating profit is not affected by the value-added sales calculation. The Company sells other metals and materials that are not considered direct pass throughs, and these costs are not deducted from net sales to calculate value-added sales.
The Company’s pricing policy is to pass the cost of these metals on to customers in order to mitigate the impact of price volatility on the Company’s results from operations. Value-added information is being presented since changes in metal prices may not directly impact profitability. It is the Company’s intent to allow users of the financial statements to review sales with and without the impact of the pass-through metals.

In addition to presenting financial statements prepared in accordance with U.S. generally accepted accounting principles (GAAP), this earnings release contains financial measures, including gross margin, segment gross margin, operating profit, segment operating profit, earnings before interest, taxes, depreciation, depletion and amortization (EBITDA), segment EBITDA, net income, and earnings per share, on a non-GAAP basis. As detailed in the above reconciliation and Attachments 6-8, we have adjusted the results for certain special items, including the following:
1.Restructuring and cost reduction – Costs include restructuring charges, costs associated with temporarily idled facilities as a result of decreased demand and costs associated with disposal of assets associated with obsolete products.
2.Product quality issue - Represents costs incurred related to a previously identified quality issue in the fourth quarter of 2025 identified by a large precision clad strip customer which led to temporarily idling production facilities within the Performance Materials segment.
3.Business transformation costs – Represents project management and implementation expenses related to the Company's automation and transformation initiatives.
4.Merger, acquisition and divestiture related costs – Includes due diligence costs associated with potential merger, acquisition and divestitures as well as loss on asset disposals.
5.Environmental remediation – Cost associated with non-recurring environmental remediation
6.Other – Costs primarily consist of miscellaneous expenses that are not indicative of current performance and are therefore included within our EBITDA adjustments, but are not significant enough to warrant separate presentation.

Internally, management reviews the results of operations without the impact of these costs in order to assess the profitability from ongoing activities. We are providing this information because we believe it will assist investors in analyzing our financial results and, when viewed in conjunction with the GAAP results, provide a more comprehensive understanding of the factors and trends affecting our operations.
