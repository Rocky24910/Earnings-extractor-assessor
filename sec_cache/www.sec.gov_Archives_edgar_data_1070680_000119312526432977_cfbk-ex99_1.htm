CF BANKSHARES INC., PARENT OF CFBANK NA, REPORTS RESULTS FOR THE 3rd QUARTER 2026.

Columbus, Ohio – October 27, 2026 – CF Bankshares Inc. (NASDAQ: CFBK) (the “Company”), the parent of CFBank, National Association (“CFBank”), today announced financial results for the third quarter ended September 30, 2026.

Third Quarter 2026 Highlights

 

•
Net income for Q3 2026 was $6.0 million ($0.91 per diluted common share), which included $551,000 of Provision expense. Pre-provision, pre-tax net revenue (PPNR) for Q3 2026 was $7.9 million.
•
Return on Average Equity (ROE) was 12.22% for Q3 2026, while Return on Average Assets (ROA) was 1.14%.
•
Net Interest Margin (NIM) was 2.96%, an increase of 3bps when compared to the prior quarter and an increase of 20bps when compared to Q3 2025.
•
Commercial Loan Fundings of $79 million in Q3, resulting in Net Commercial Loan growth of $42 million for the quarter. Year-to-date commercial loan fundings have totaled $357 million with $129 million in Net Commercial Loan growth.
•
Customer Fees, including Treasury Management products and services increased $71,000, or 8%, when compared to the prior quarter and have increased $660,000, or 32% year-to-date in 2026 when compared to the same period in 2025.
•
Book value per share increased to $29.83 as of September 30, 2026, representing a 10.5% increase year-over-year.
•
CFBank’s capital position remains strong with a Tier 1 Leverage Ratio of 11.99% and a Total Capital Ratio of 14.69%.

Recent Developments

•
On October 1, 2026, the Company’s Board of Directors declared a cash dividend of $0.09 per share on its Common Stock. The dividend was paid on October 20, 2026 to shareholders of record as of the close of business on October 12, 2026.

Overview of Results

Net income for the three months ended September 30, 2026 totaled $6.0 million (or $0.91 per diluted common share) compared to net income of $5.9 million (or $0.90 per diluted common share) for the three months ended June 30, 2026 and net income of $2.3 million (or $0.36 per diluted common share) for the three months ended September 30, 2025. PPNR for the three months ended September 30, 2026 was $7.9 million compared to PPNR of $8.2 million for the three months ended June 30, 2026 and PPNR of $7.8 million for the three months ended September 30, 2025.

Net income for the nine months ended September 30, 2026 totaled $17.0 million (or $2.59 per diluted common share) compared to net income of $11.8 million (or $1.81 per diluted common share) for the nine months ended September 30, 2025. PPNR for the nine months ended September 30, 2026 was $22.6 million compared to PPNR of $21.8 million for the nine months ended September 30, 2025.

Net Interest Income and Net Interest Margin

Net interest income totaled $14.9 million for the quarter ended September 30, 2026 and increased $105,000, or 0.7%, compared to $14.8 million for the prior quarter, and increased $1.1 million, or 8.4%, compared to $13.8 million for the third quarter of 2025.

The increase in net interest income compared to the prior quarter was primarily due to a $92,000, or 0.3%, increase in interest income, coupled with a $13,000, or 0.1%, decrease in interest expense. The increase in interest income was primarily attributed to a $37.4 million, or 2.1%, increase in average loans and leases, coupled with a 5bps increase in the average yield on interest-earning assets. The decrease in interest expense when compared to the prior quarter was attributed to a 7bps decrease in the average rate on interest-bearing liabilities, partially offset by a $26.8 million, or 1.7%, increase in average interest-bearing liabilities. The net interest margin of 2.96% for the quarter ended September 30, 2026 increased 3bps compared to the net interest margin of 2.93% for the prior quarter.

The increase in net interest income compared to the third quarter of 2025 was primarily due to a $1.4 million, or 8.5%, decrease in interest expense, partially offset by a $255,000, or 0.8%, decrease in interest income. The decrease in interest expense was primarily attributed to a 40bps decrease in the average rate on interest-bearing liabilities, partially offset by a $20.5 million, or 1.3%, increase in average interest-bearing liabilities. The decrease in interest income was primarily attributed to a 11bps decrease in the average yield on interest-earning assets, partially offset by a $17.7 million, or 0.9%, increase in average interest-earning assets outstanding. The net interest margin of 2.96% for the quarter ended September 30, 2026 increased 20bps compared to the net interest margin of 2.76% for the third quarter of 2025.

Noninterest income for the three months ended September 30, 2026 totaled $1.8 million and increased $58,000, or 3.4%, compared to $1.7 million for the prior quarter and increased $41,000, or 2.4%, compared to $1.7 million for the three months ended September 30, 2025.

Noninterest expense for the quarter ended September 30, 2026 totaled $8.8 million and increased $426,000, or 5.1%, compared to $8.3 million for the prior quarter, and increased $1.1 million, or 13.4%, compared to $7.7 million for the quarter ended September 30, 2025. The increase in noninterest expense when compared to the prior quarter was driven by a $264,000 increase in recruiting

 

expenses related to the expansion of our Commercial Banking Teams across our Regional Markets. During the third quarter, one-time expenses included $111,000 in signing bonuses and $198,000 in recruiting fees.

The increase in noninterest expense when compared to the third quarter of 2025 was driven by a $751,000 increase in salaries and benefits, which was driven by the expansion of our Commercial Banking Teams across our Regional Markets.

Income tax expense was $1.4 million for the quarter ended September 30, 2026 (effective tax rate of 18.4%), compared to $1.4 million for the prior quarter (effective tax rate of 18.6%) and $373,000 for the quarter ended September 30, 2025 (effective tax rate of 13.8%).

Asset Quality

Nonaccrual loans were $20.4 million, or 1.10% of total loans, at September 30, 2026, a decrease of $478,000 from $20.8 million at June 30, 2026 and an increase of $5.0 million from $15.3 million at December 31, 2025. The increase in nonperforming loans when compared to December 31, 2025 included the addition of one non-core (non-customer) C&I loan for $5.0 million. Of the $20.4 million of nonaccrual loans at September 30, 2026, $4.8 million was guaranteed by the SBA.

Loans 30 days or more past due totaled $30.8 million at September 30, 2026, compared to $20.4 million at June 30, 2026 and $12.9 million at December 31, 2025. The increase in loans 30 days or more past due when compared to the previous quarter was primarily due to a $6.7 million C&I loan and a $5.9 million commercial real estate loan (one relationship). Subsequent to quarter end, both of these loans were brought current.

The allowance for credit losses on loans and leases totaled $19.0 million at September 30, 2026, compared to $19.4 million at June 30, 2026 and $17.7 million at December 31, 2025. The ratio of the allowance for credit losses on loans and leases to total loans and leases was 1.03% at September 30, 2026 compared to 1.07% at June 30, 2026 and 1.01% at December 31, 2025.

 

There was $551,000 in provision for credit losses expense for the quarter ended September 30, 2026, compared to $944,000 for the quarter ended June 30, 2026 and $5.1 million for the quarter ended September 30, 2025. Net charge-offs for the quarter ended September 30, 2026 totaled $966,000, compared to net recoveries of $106,000 for the prior quarter and net charge-offs of $7.1 million for the quarter ended September 30, 2025. Net charge-offs during the quarter ended September 30, 2026 were attributed to credits that had already been identified and fully reserved in previous quarters. As a result, these charge-offs had no impact on third quarter 2026 provision expense.

Deposits

Deposits totaled $1.9 billion at September 30, 2026, an increase of $28.5 million, or 1.6%, from June 30, 2026, and an increase of $76.5 million, or 4.3%, from December 31, 2025. The increase when compared to the prior quarter was primarily due to a $29.5 million increase in interest-bearing account balances, partially offset by a $922,000 decrease in noninterest-bearing account balances. The increase when compared to December 31, 2025 was primarily due to a $89.3 million increase in interest-bearing account balances, partially offset by a $12.8 million decrease in noninterest-bearing accounts balances.

At September 30, 2026, approximately 31.2% of our deposit balances exceeded the FDIC insurance limit of $250,000, as compared to approximately 32.0% at June 30, 2026 and approximately 29.5% at December 31, 2025.

Borrowings

FHLB advances and other debt totaled $99.2 million at September 30, 2026 and June 30, 2026 and $101.0 million at December 31, 2025.

Capital

Stockholders’ equity totaled $199.5 million at September 30, 2026, an increase of $5.2 million, or 2.7%, when compared to $194.3 million at June 30, 2026, and an increase of $15.1 million, or 8.2%, from $184.4 million at December 31, 2025. The increase in total stockholders’ equity during the three months ended September 30, 2026 was primarily attributed to net income, partially offset by $588,000 in dividend payments.

Use of Non-GAAP Financial Measures

This earnings release contains financial information and performance measures determined by methods other than in accordance with accounting principles generally accepted in the United States of America (“GAAP”). Non-GAAP financial measures included in this earnings release include Pre-Provision, Pre-Tax Net Revenue (PPNR). PPNR is defined as net interest income plus total non-interest income, excluding net gains and losses, minus total non-interest expense. This measure is a non-GAAP financial measure because it excludes the provision for (recovery of) credit losses and all gains and losses included in net income. Management uses this "non-GAAP" financial measure in its analysis of the Company’s performance and believes that this non-GAAP financial measure provides a greater understanding of ongoing operations and enhances comparability of results with prior periods and peers.

Disclosures of non-GAAP financial measures should not be viewed as substitutes for financial measures determined in accordance with GAAP, nor are they necessarily comparable to non-GAAP performance measures that may be presented by other companies. A reconciliation of this non-GAAP financial measure to the most directly comparable GAAP financial measure is included at the end of this earnings release under the heading "GAAP TO NON-GAAP RECONCILIATION."

About CF Bankshares Inc. and CFBank

CF Bankshares Inc. (the “Company”) is a bank holding company that owns 100% of the stock of CFBank, National Association (“CFBank”). CFBank is a nationally chartered boutique Commercial bank operating primarily in Five (5) Major Metro Markets: Columbus, Cleveland, Cincinnati, and Akron Ohio, and Indianapolis, Indiana. The current Leadership Team and Board recapitalized the Company and CFBank in 2012 during the financial crisis, repositioning CFBank as a full-service Commercial Bank model.

CFBank focuses on serving the financial needs of closely held businesses and entrepreneurs, by providing a comprehensive Commercial, Retail, and Mortgage Lending services presence. In all regional markets, CFBank provides commercial loans and equipment leases, commercial and residential real estate loans and treasury management depository services, residential mortgage lending, and full-service commercial and retail banking services and products. CFBank is differentiated by our penchant for individualized service coupled with direct customer access to decision-makers, and ease of doing business. CFBank matches the sophistication of much larger banks, without the bureaucracy.
