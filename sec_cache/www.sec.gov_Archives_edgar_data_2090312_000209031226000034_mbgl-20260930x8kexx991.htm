MOBILITY GLOBAL REPORTS THIRD QUARTER 2026 FINANCIAL RESULTS
CENTREVILLE, Va., November 12, 2026 - Mobility Global Inc. (NYSE: MBGL) (“Mobility Global” or the
“Company”) today announced financial results for the third quarter ended September 30, 2026.
•Grew revenue 7.4% year-over-year, or 7.6% on an organic constant currency1 basis, a sequential step-
up from the first half
•Grew both segments with CARFAX revenue up 8% to $313 million and B2B revenue up 6% to $164
million
•Reported net income of $42 million and adjusted EBITDA¹ of $195 million, inclusive of standalone
public company costs for the first time
•Authorized an $800 million share repurchase program, with buybacks expected to begin before year end
and $350-$450 million of repurchases targeted through year-end 2027
•Raised full-year 2026 adjusted EBITDA guidance midpoint while maintaining full-year revenue
guidance
“Our first full quarter as a standalone public company was a solid one, with growth stepping up from the first
half across both segments," said Bill Eager, Chief Executive Officer of Mobility Global. “We are bringing our
CARFAX and B2B businesses together as One Mobility Global and applying AI to our proprietary data, trusted
brands and deeply embedded customer relationships to deliver more value to our customers and drive growth.
We believe our strong cash generation enables us to invest in these priorities while returning capital to
shareholders through our dividend and share repurchase program. We remain focused on disciplined execution,
finishing the year strong and building momentum into 2027.”
Third Quarter Financial Highlights
(All comparisons are to the third quarter of 2025)
•Total revenue of $478 million, increased by 7.4%
•Subscription revenue increased by 7% and non-subscription revenue also increased by 7%
•CARFAX segment revenue of $313 million, increased by 8%
•B2B segment revenue of $164 million, increased by 6%
•Net income of $42 million, decreased by 41% and represented a 9% margin
•Adjusted EBITDA¹ of $195 million, increased by 2% and represented a 41% margin

Third Quarter 2026 and Recent Business Highlights
•Added a large global OEM to B2B Sales Solutions, a win made possible by operating as One Mobility
Global.
•Expanded CARFAX’s data estate and consumer reach since year-end 2025, reaching milestones of
more than 100,000 service shops (up approximately 8,000), 60 million Car Care consumers (up
approximately 7 million), and more than 40 billion vehicle history records (up approximately 2 billion).
•Expanded CARFAX Homegrown across all CARFAX dealer and consumer assets, including CARFAX
Listings, Car Care and dealer tools. CARFAX Homegrown gives consumers more ways to find vehicles
sold and serviced by the same dealership, and dealers more ways to acquire and sell that inventory.
•Paid first quarterly cash dividend of $0.06 per share, and in October the Board declared the next
quarterly dividend of $0.06 per share.
•In October, the Board authorized an $800 million share repurchase program, with buybacks expected to
begin before year end and $350-$450 million of repurchases targeted through year-end 2027.

Declaration of Quarterly Dividend
The Board of Directors has declared a quarterly common stock dividend of $0.06 per share. The dividend is
payable on December 14, 2026, to shareholders of record at the close of business as of November 30, 2026. The
annualized dividend rate is $0.24 per share.
Share Repurchase Program
In October 2026, the Board of Directors authorized a share repurchase program to repurchase up to $800
million in shares of the Company’s common stock, from time-to-time on the open market, in privately
negotiated transactions, or otherwise, as determined by the Company’s management in its discretion. The
Company currently expects to begin repurchases before the end of 2026 and to repurchase $350 to $450 million
of common stock by the end of 2027, subject to management's evaluation of market conditions, applicable legal
requirements and other factors.

Diluted earnings per share, and Free cash flow
Organic Constant Currency is a non-GAAP measure and is defined as our GAAP revenue adjusted to exclude
the impact of foreign currency translation and of acquisitions and divestitures. Adjusted EBITDA is a non-
GAAP measure and is defined as our GAAP net income adjusted to exclude (1) interest expense and other, net,
(2) provisions for income taxes, (3) depreciation and amortization, (4) stock-based compensation, (5) 
transaction costs related to the stand-up of the Spin Business in connection with the Separation, and (6)
employee severance charges and other costs that are not representative of the underlying economics of the
periods presented. Net income is the most directly comparable GAAP financial measure to Adjusted EBITDA.
Adjusted EBITDA Margin is a non-GAAP measure and refers to Adjusted EBITDA divided by GAAP revenue.
Adjusted Net income is a non-GAAP measure and is defined as our GAAP net income adjusted to exclude (1)
amortization of intangibles, (2) stock-based compensation, (3) separation-related and transaction costs related to
the stand-up of the Spin Business in connection with the Separation, (4) employee severance charges and other
costs that are not representative of the underlying economics of the periods presented, and (5) tax effects of the
adjustments listed above using an assumed effective tax rate. Net income is the most directly comparable GAAP
financial measure to Adjusted Net income. Adjusted diluted earnings per share is a non-GAAP measure and
refers to diluted earnings per share calculated using Adjusted Net income as opposed to GAAP net income. Free
cash flow is a non-GAAP financial measure and reflects our cash provided by operating activities less capital
expenditures.
As used herein, the “Spin Business” refers to the business of S&P Global and its subsidiaries with respect to
providing analytics, marketing, planning solutions, reports, forecasts and vehicle history data for the automotive
sector, which, prior to the Separation, operated under the S&P Global Mobility division.  The term “Separation”
refers to the separation of the Spin Business from S&P Global and the creation of an independent, publicly
traded company, Mobility Global.
