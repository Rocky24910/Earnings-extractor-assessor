ALBUQUERQUE, NM — (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) — ARRAY Technologies, Inc. (NASDAQ: ARRY) (“ARRAY” or the “Company”), a leading global provider of integrated energy infrastructure products, software, and service solutions, today announced financial results for its third quarter ended September 30, 2026.

“The third quarter marked another significant milestone in ARRAY’s evolution from a tracker company to a provider of integrated energy infrastructure solutions. With the completion of our Affordable Wire Management acquisition, we advanced our balance-of-system strategy and gained access to the fast-growing battery energy storage and data center markets. Combined with the formal launch of ARRAY Atlas™ I and II and our new 60-degree U.S. tracker variant, we are delivering on our strategy to offer customers a more complete, interoperable solution set. This is translating into strong commercial momentum, with a record orderbook of $2.6 billion, up 42% year-over-year, and a trailing twelve-month book-to-bill of 1.6x,” said Chief Executive Officer, Kevin G. Hostetler.

Mr. Hostetler continued, “While our updated full-year guidance reflects shifts in the timing of certain project deliveries, underlying customer demand remains strong and our continued operational execution and cost discipline are driving improved gross margin expectations. With a record orderbook and an expanded portfolio, we are entering 2027 with strong momentum and we believe we are well positioned to deliver profitable growth and create long-term shareholder value.”

Updating Full Year 2026 Guidance

We now expect full-year Adjusted Gross Margin(2) to be approximately 27%. As a result, for the year ending December 31, 2026, the Company now expects:

 

  •  

Revenue to be in the range of $1.34 billion to $1.40 billion, previously $1.4 billion to $1.5 billion

 

  •  

Adjusted EBITDA(2) to be in the range of $190 million to $210 million, previously $210 million to $230 million

 

  •  

Adjusted net income per common share(2) to be in the range of $0.57 to $0.69, previously $0.68 to $0.75

For the quarter ending December 31, 2026, the Company expects revenue to be in the range of $445 million to $505 million.

About ARRAY Technologies, Inc.

ARRAY Technologies (NASDAQ: ARRY) is a leading global provider of integrated energy infrastructure for utility-scale and distributed generation providers. Engineered for interoperability, ARRAY’s innovative hardware, software, and services span solar trackers, fixed-tilt systems, foundations, wire management, and other balance-of-system solutions. ARRAY’s products work together to maximize energy production, withstand harsh weather conditions, and deliver value for the entire lifecycle of a project. Non-GAAP Financial Information

This press release includes certain financial measures that are not presented in accordance with U.S. generally accepted accounting principles (“GAAP”), including Adjusted gross profit, Adjusted gross margin, Adjusted EBITDA, Adjusted net income, Adjusted net income per common share, Adjusted general and administrative expense, and Free cash flow.

We define Adjusted gross profit as gross profit plus (i) amortization of developed technology and backlog and (ii) acquisition-related expenses. We define Adjusted gross margin as Adjusted gross profit as a percentage of revenue. We define Adjusted EBITDA as net income (loss) to common stockholders plus (i) other income, net, (ii) gain on extinguishment of debts, net, (iii) foreign currency (gain) loss, net, (iv) preferred dividends and accretion, (v) interest expense, (vi) income tax expense, (vii) depreciation expense, (viii) amortization of intangibles, (ix) amortization of developed technology and backlog, (x) equity-based compensation, (xi) change in fair value of contingent consideration, (xii) certain legal expenses, and (xiii) acquisition-related expenses. We define Adjusted net income as net income (loss) to common stockholders plus (i) amortization of intangibles, (ii) amortization of developed technology and backlog, (iii) amortization of debt discount and issuance costs, (iv) gain on extinguishment of debts, net (v) Series A preferred stock accretion, (vi) equity-based compensation, (vii) change in fair value of contingent consideration, (viii) certain legal expenses, (ix) acquisition-related expenses, and (x) income tax expense adjustments. We define Adjusted general and administrative expense as general and administrative expense less (i) equity-based compensation, (ii) certain legal expenses, and (iii) acquisition-related expenses. We define Free cash flow as Net cash provided by operating activities less purchase of property, plant and equipment.
